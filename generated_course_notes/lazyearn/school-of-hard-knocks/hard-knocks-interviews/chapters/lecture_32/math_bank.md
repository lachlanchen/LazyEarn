1. Take the spoken personal sale outcome \(E_{\mathrm{Ann}} \approx \$90\,\mathrm{M}\).

4. Compute \(s_{\mathrm{Ann}} \approx 0.30\).
5. State this only as rough editorial arithmetic, not as audited cap-table fact.

Recurring-revenue preference

1. Model each repeat payment as \(r_t\).
2. Sum the payment stream as \(R_{\mathrm{rec}}=\sum_{t=1}^{T} r_t\).
3. Compare this to a one-off sale with only one payment event.
4. Preserve the lecture’s claim that repeated inflows are the preferred wealth engine.
5. Use the tanning/gym/Netflix examples as transcript evidence, not as separate proof.

Owner-financed acquisition

1. Let the total purchase price be \(P\).
2. Let the buyer bring little or no cash \(D\).

4. Write the structure as \(P=D+F_{\mathrm{seller}}\).
5. Tie the mechanism to the “Silver Tsunami” condition \(B_{\mathrm{sale}} > B_{\mathrm{buyers}}\).
