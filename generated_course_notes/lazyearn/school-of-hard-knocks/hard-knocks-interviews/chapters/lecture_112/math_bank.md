## Derivation Steps
Scaling threshold
1. Start with the lecture’s explicit contrast: a six-figure business can be run by one person.
2. Treat that as a solo-capacity regime, \(T_{\text{solo}}\).
3. Crossing into seven figures introduces training, delegation, patience, and trust.
4. Therefore scale is not just more effort; it is a shift to \(T_{\text{team}} > T_{\text{solo}}\).

Sales and market knowledge
1. The software CEO says to know the marketplace better than the technology.
2. Buyer research comes before the pitch.
3. Belief in the product is required or hesitation appears in the pitch.
4. Directness matters because vague talk wastes the buyer’s time.
5. Immediate scheduling of the next step reduces follow-up slippage.
6. Therefore selling ability is modeled as disciplined market process, not mere product quality.

Equity and wealth
1. The lecture distinguishes earning from owning.
2. Ownership is introduced through equity, including sweat equity.
3. If value accrues to the company and the speaker holds \(\alpha\), personal upside tracks that share.
4. Hence the working reconstruction \(W_{\text{equity}} \propto \alpha V\).

Investability and durability
1. The insurance operator states that no one wants to invest in a shrinking business.
2. Encode that as the sign condition \(g>0\).
3. Retention of good people signals organizational durability.
4. Repeated small operational excellences create perceived quality.
5. Therefore growth plus retention plus execution quality form the durability package.

Real-estate discipline
1. Begin with the rule: do not spend what you do not have to get what you do not need.
2. Translate that into a low-leverage stance.
3. Financial stress degrades decision quality.
4. Copy successful operators and proceed step by step.
5. The “two point game” metaphor means compounding many modest wins rather than waiting for rare large wins.

Referral flywheel
1. No clients means no work.
2. High-quality service keeps clients satisfied.
3. Satisfied clients generate referrals.
4. Referrals reduce reliance on constant prospecting.
5. Therefore service quality compounds commercially through \(Q_{\text{service}}\uparrow \Rightarrow R_{\text{referrals}}\uparrow\).

Creator monetization order
1. Attention is not yet monetization.
2. Start with affiliate marketing.
3. Then build owned offers, services, courses, or merch.
4. Brand deals come later, not first.
5. Ego distorts this sequence by making validation outrun business judgment.

AI leverage
1. AI first appears as a simple licensable utility.
2. It later appears as an assistant that systemizes existing work.
3. That reduces routine time burden, \(t_{\text{ops}}\downarrow\).
4. Reduced friction increases usable productive capacity, \(E_{\text{throughput}}\uparrow\).
5. The released capacity is then redirected toward more clients or more valuable work.
