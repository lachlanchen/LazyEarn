## Definitions And Objects
- \(N_{\text{trucks}}\): Collins’s operating scale; use as a scale marker, not an audited fleet count.
- \(R_{\max}\): Collins’s stated single-year revenue band.
- \(T_t\): accumulated trust/reputation from repeated service performance.
- \(\Delta T_t\): trust increment produced by doing what one says, including recovery when problems occur.
- \(B_t\): accumulated built enterprise/career stock in the Montana “turn the crank” segment.
- \(\Delta B_t\): incremental gain from sustained work over long horizons.
- \(h_{\text{work}}\): reported daily work hours in the early tech phase.
- \(s_{\text{sleep}}\): reported nightly sleep in that same phase.
- \(D(t)\): national debt path; conceptual, not calibrated.
- \(D'(t)\): borrowing trajectory / slope of debt growth, not an interest rate.
- \(P_{\text{pp}}(t)\): purchasing power of ordinary Americans.
- \(I_{\text{member}}\): information available to a member of Congress.
- \(I_{\text{public}}\): information available to the general public.
- \(V\): coalition vote count behind a proposal.
- \(V_{\text{majority}}\): threshold needed to move something on the floor.
- \(N_{\text{House}}\): rough institutional scale marker from Meeks’s “500 or so people.”
- \(a\): age marker in the Donalds life-course advice.
- \(S_{20^-},S_{20^+}\): personal state before and after the explicit life-decision threshold at age 20.

## Derivation Steps
Collins first-asset path
1. Begin with no large inherited productive asset stated in the transcript.
2. A community bank knows the borrower locally.
3. That local knowledge supports first-truck financing.
4. The truck creates operating capacity.
5. Operating capacity plus execution allows expansion from \(1\) to \(100+\).

Collins service-to-scale path
1. The firm is not the biggest.
2. It delivers what it says it will deliver.
3. When things go wrong, it compensates and re-routes.
4. Customers observe reliability repeatedly.
5. Reliability raises \(T_t\).
6. Higher \(T_t\) produces repeat demand and scale.

Meeks trajectory path
1. Start in public housing and poverty.
2. Prioritize education.
3. Add reading and awareness of the surrounding environment.
4. Convert awareness into contribution and work with others.
5. Contribution becomes a path into office and public action.

Montana long-horizon build path
1. Move from poor origin to college to NYU research work.
2. Form a startup.
3. Enter a pooling-of-interests merger with Yahoo.
4. Recycle gains into angel financing and early-stage tech support.
5. Keep “turning the crank” rather than waiting for an instant jump.
6. After years of repeated increments, look back at what has been built.

Donalds debt path
1. Borrowing continues.
2. Rising borrowing raises the cost burden.
3. Higher debt burden erodes purchasing power.
4. Poor households and seniors on fixed incomes absorb early damage.
5. Immediate target is a smaller \(D'(t)\), not instant debt reversal.
6. Only after slope control does long-horizon paydown become thinkable.

Donalds institutional-power path
1. Titles matter, but floor action still depends on counted votes.
2. A clustered bloc willing to hold firm changes the effective count.
3. If \(V\ge V_{\text{majority}}\), motion is possible.
4. If \(V<V_{\text{majority}}\), nothing moves.
5. Real operational power is therefore coalition arithmetic, not title alone.

Donalds self-command path
1. Low points occur at ages 18 and 20.
2. A mirror-level decision is made at 20.
3. Early years become grind and skill formation.
4. Advice at 29 sets a target horizon of 40.
5. Faith stabilizes the path.
6. The anti-rigged conclusion is: run the play rather than surrender the game.

## Notation Choices
- Use \(t\) for discrete periods of business or political accumulation.
- Use \(\to\) for causal progression, ladder movement, or state transition; do not use \(=\) for those moves.
- Use \(R_{\max}\) for Collins’s revenue band rather than inventing profit notation.
- Use \(N_{\text{trucks}}\) as the main productive-asset scale variable in the Collins section.
- Use \(T_t\) for trust/reputation only as editorial shorthand; mark it as reconstructed, not spoken notation.
- Use \(B_t\) for long-horizon building only in the Montana section; keep it generic and cumulative.
- Use \(D(t)\) for debt level and \(D'(t)\) for borrowing trajectory; do not introduce extra macro variables unless later needed.
- Use \(P_{\text{pp}}(t)\) for purchasing power to avoid confusion with power in Washington.
- Use \(I_{\text{member}}, I_{\text{public}}\) as information sets in the ethics section.
- Use \(V_{\text{majority}}\) generically; do not hard-code chamber thresholds unless a later prompt asks for civics precision.
- Use explicit ages \(20,21,29,40,46,70\) where the lecture itself foregrounds them.
- Keep USD magnitudes in interval form when only approximate bands are spoken.
- Avoid formal operator notation from physics or heavy economics notation; this lecture supports causal schematics, not dense formalism.
