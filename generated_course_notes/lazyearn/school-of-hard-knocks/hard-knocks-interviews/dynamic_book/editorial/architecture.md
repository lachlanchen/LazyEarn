# How You Got Rich Architecture

Status: evidence-map stage. Titles and boundaries remain provisional until all
135 sources have substantive placements.

## Narrative Spine

The book begins with the desire for money, follows value into ownership and
distribution, enters the operating and financial machinery that makes wealth
durable, and ends by asking what freedom is for. Happiness is not an appendix;
it is the criterion that tests every earlier promise.

## Provisional Parts

### Part I: What Money Is For

Define rich, wealthy, secure, and free. Establish the interviews as varied
experience rather than universal law. Introduce desire, geography, access,
starting conditions, luck, and the costs hidden by visible outcomes.

### Part II: Make Something Valuable

Develop customer need, problem selection, product design, sales, communication,
marketing, attention, reputation, and distribution. Show how ordinary or
unfashionable work becomes economically important.

### Part III: Own the Machine

Move from labor and expertise into equity, recurring revenue, transferable
systems, teams, leadership, partnerships, culture, and succession. Preserve
employee ownership and professional-practice routes alongside founder stories.

### Part IV: Capital, Risk, and Time

Explain cash flow, retained earnings, compounding, public markets, real estate,
debt, taxes, insurance, legal shells, portfolio stages, and downside survival.
Separate interview doctrine from generally defensible financial reasoning.

### Part V: Enough

Return to health, family, integrity, service, meaning, faith, regret, and the
finite interval of life. Wealth should culminate in usable freedom, responsible
leadership, and a life whose value is not measured by the number alone.

## Architecture Gate

Final chapter contracts will be written only after each source has at least one
primary placement, any necessary secondary placement, and an explicit tension
or limitation where the advice could otherwise become misleading.
