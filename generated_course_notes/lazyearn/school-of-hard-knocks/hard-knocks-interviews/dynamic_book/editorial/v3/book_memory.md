## Live Argument

The book begins with the job money must continue to perform, not a status
number. Material sufficiency comes first because relief from insecurity changes
the feasible life. Practical freedom requires durable claims, capabilities,
relationships, time, and margin when present income stops. Further accumulation
must then answer what it is for.

Abundance means sufficient resources, capability, relationships, time, and
options. Fulfillment concerns their direction toward work and relationships
worth the effort. Contentment is the retained authority to recognize enough
and stop enlarging a game that no longer serves the life. None substitutes for
the others, and contentment cannot excuse deprivation.

comparison. Chapter 23 must revise rather than repeat this record.

Chapter 2 adds the starting position to Record 1: usable resources, buildable
capability, missing access and counterparties, structural constraints and
specific allies, and one bounded next move with a maximum loss and review date.
The record must keep peace, public infrastructure, institutions, and household
care visible without using structural limits to erase the next available
decision.

Chapter 3 carries that bounded move into a first-margin cycle: one capability
and promised result, a short proof cycle, a named buyer, household margin, a
reserve with one job, and a review date with explicit continue, change, and
stop evidence. A multigenerational horizon enters only as a reason to leave
later people options rather than present status claims.
