# V3 Whole-Book Review

Status: final. The full-size and 1.2x pocket editions are accepted, published,
released, and synchronized with verified checksums.

## Argument and Continuity

- The five-part progression holds: define the life and material floor; create
  value with evidence; turn useful work into an owned, transferable system;
  protect capital across risk and time; then decide what freedom and enough
  are for.
- The abundance, fulfillment, and contentment arc develops through decisions
  rather than recurring slogans. Financial freedom remains usable agency, not
  a fourth prize or an escape from ordinary life.
- The five living records accumulate across the manuscript. Later exercises
  revise earlier decisions instead of opening twenty-three unrelated
  worksheets.
- Recurring cases return only when their narrative job changes. Contradictory
  claims remain bounded by incentives, starting conditions, survivorship,
  consent, legal duties, downside, and the limits of personal experience.
- The ending resolves the opening question through a material floor, positive
  uses of freedom, stewardship, and a stopping judgment without becoming
  anti-wealth or promising that money solves every human problem.

## Voice and Repetition

- A whole-book cadence pass removed the one exact cross-chapter sentence
  duplicate and reduced the predictable `not ... . It is ...` correction
  pattern from 49 instances to 27 where contrast still performs real work.
- No exact prose sentence is repeated across chapters. No content-level
  eight-word sequence appears in three or more chapters.
- The revised passages in the front matter and Chapters 2--5 and 21--23 retain
  every claim, story, qualification, decision, and exercise while replacing
  mechanical correction language with direct prose.
- Extracted reader text contains no private workflow, model, session, path, or
