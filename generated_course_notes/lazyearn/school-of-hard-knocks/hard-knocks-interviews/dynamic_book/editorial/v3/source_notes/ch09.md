## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

V3 uses one operating flow:

`attention -> qualified decision -> exchange -> delivery -> acceptance or
complaint -> collection or repair -> return, referral, or exit`.

At each handoff the record names the responsible person, promised and actual
waiting time, current constraint, and person who waits, pays, or bears risk.
The slowest necessary stage limits the promise. Increasing attention at a
delivery bottleneck enlarges the queue rather than the business.

The chapter retains the contribution expression as a decision discipline, not
a universal lifetime-value formula. It includes acquisition, variable
delivery, service, recovery, retention probability, refunds, uncertainty, and
a defensible horizon. Record 2 closes with channel and trust evidence, then
hands one route identifier and unresolved constraint to Record 3 instead of
creating a separate distribution worksheet.
