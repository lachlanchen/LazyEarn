## Explanatory Sources

- *The Psychology of Money* informed the comparison, time-control, luck, and
  ruin boundaries. Its anecdotes are not imported.
- *Your Money or Your Life*, Chapter 4, informed the distinction between price
  and the earning, recovery, maintenance, and attention that an expense asks.
  The chapter's audit is an original, narrower adaptation.
- The *Daodejing*, chapters 32, 33, 44, and 46, informed the separation between
  recognizing sufficiency and retaining the authority to stop.
- *Mencius*, especially *King Hui of Liang I* and *Teng Wen Gong I*, informed
  the boundary that contentment cannot excuse material insecurity.
- Krishnamurti and Alan Anderson's discussion pg6xdfYe6To informed the
  narrower observation that remembered reward and comparison can turn an
  enjoyed experience into repeated pursuit. The stronger philosophical claim
  is not adopted.

These works clarify mechanisms and limits; they do not corroborate interview
facts. No outside anecdote or unchecked quotation appears in the chapter.
