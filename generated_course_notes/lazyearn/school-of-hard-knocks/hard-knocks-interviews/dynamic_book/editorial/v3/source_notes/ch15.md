## Focused Research Synthesis

Selected Huainanzi passages sharpened the constructive succession mechanism:
people differ in fitness, no one contains every craft, teaching can preserve
knowledge, and useful action must adapt to changed conditions. Hierarchy,
coercion, forced training, and inherited political premises are rejected.

Thomas Mann's Buddenbrooks supplied an invisible diagnostic boundary: a family
name, firm, house, office, and expected heir can become one burden after fit and
willingness disappear. The novel is not summarized or treated as evidence that
family firms inevitably decline. Its useful question is whether continuity
serves living people and a current promise or conscripts a descendant into a
monument.

The Great Gatsby and Father Goriot remain reserved for later questions about
status, purchased affection, and enough. No literary title or character is
used decoratively in the reader chapter. No current trust, inheritance, tax,
beneficiary, or estate instruction is supplied without jurisdiction-specific
primary review.

## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter separates two sets of concepts. Continuity, succession, transfer,
and sale describe what can happen to the enterprise. Kinship, ownership,
employment, and authority describe relationships people can hold to it. A
willing and capable successor must also receive legitimate authority, retain
challenge and removal, renew nonassignable trust, and have a fallback if the
fit fails.

Material provision and cultivated capability remain complementary. Declining a
family occupation is not betrayal, and a qualified manager, employee group,
partner, outside buyer, narrower operation, or orderly closure may preserve
more value than a reluctant heir.

Record 3 receives one integrated transferability and succession review:
purpose and protected people; demand and economics; rights and obligations;
roles and candidates; transition evidence; trust and choice; transaction
bridge; and failure and review. Record 5 opens only family purpose, protected
people, role boundaries, and freedom to decline. The later inheritance and
enough chapters keep the broader family compact.

## Figure Decision

The primary and recurring interviews offer generic street, home, and
talking-head frames rather than documentary evidence of a succession process,
rights transfer, operating review, or trust record. A status setting would
weaken the chapter's distinction between price and stewardship. Chapter 15
therefore uses no figure.
