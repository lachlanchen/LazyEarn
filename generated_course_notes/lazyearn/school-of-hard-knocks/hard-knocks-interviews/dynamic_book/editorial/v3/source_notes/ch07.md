## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The buyer-value equation, accelerated-time estimate, seller-floor and
buyer-ceiling ranges, scenario discipline, and expected-value expression remain
original explanatory models rather than promises of measurable precision. V3
adds three controls:

1. positioning includes buyer, real alternative, promised change, proof, and
   uncertainty;
2. the price claim is attached to Chapter 6's actual evidence rung, role, and
   reproduced conditions rather than supported by a second ladder; and
3. one reciprocal exchange record separates decision, evidence step,
   commercial terms, and consent.

The record includes value range, assumptions, delivery floor, price range,
scope, timing, responsibility, remedy, review, exit, material disclosure,
affected people, safeguards, and the smallest next step useful to both sides.
Consent remains revocable until the relevant commitment and never converts
urgency into permission to manufacture fear.
