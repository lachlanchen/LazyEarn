## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter applies one two-part absence test to every stage of Chapter 9's
operating route:

- expected work records the invariant, reason, interface, outcome owner,
  measure, inspection, trigger, inputs, resources, completion, and backup;
- an unexpected condition records boundaries, stop and decision rights,
  escalation, support, expiry of temporary authority, and review.

Documentation preserves judgment and enables revision; it does not pretend to
remove judgment. Delegation climbs the same evidence ladder as the offer, from
a specific reversible decision to wider consequence only after observed
results, customer response, authority, context, resources, and support justify
the next step. Voluntary renewal remains repeated-exchange evidence rather
than proof of loyalty.

Record 3 adds accountable outcomes, ordinary and exception authority,
resources, inspection, direct customer feedback, founder interruptions, the
unresolved constraint, and permission to grow. No critical zero may be averaged
away, and a successful planned absence supports only a bounded increase.
