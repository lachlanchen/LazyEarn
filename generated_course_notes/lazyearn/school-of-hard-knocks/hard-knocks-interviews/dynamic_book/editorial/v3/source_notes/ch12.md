## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter separates period flows from dated stocks before introducing the
weekly cash calendar. Even among stocks, available cash, disputed receivables,
specialized inventory, and illiquid assets do not carry next week's obligations
equally. The peak-carry equations are authorial diagnostic tools, not equations
shown or claimed by an interviewee.

One coupled growth argument keeps demand, collected contribution, capacity,
dated cash, legitimate duties, and recoverability together. Safety, quality,
fair work, privacy, maintenance, and repair remain real costs even when no
incremental sale can be attributed to them. Present output is distinguished
from future capability so training, maintenance, tooling, and resilience may be
evaluated without calling every current loss an investment.

The timing gap always has a carrier with rights and downside. The chapter names
retained earnings, customer and supplier terms, debt, factoring, equity, and
staging without declaring a universal winner. The final capital choice is
deferred to Chapter 14, where duration, control, guarantees, pace, information,
loss allocation, and alternatives can be compared against the productive
constraint.

Record 3 receives one growth-permission decision: diagnosis, evidence,
coherent change, peak carry and carrier, boundaries, stress and stop
conditions, and the observed result required for another stage. Record 1
changes only when portable capability or the protected household floor changes.
