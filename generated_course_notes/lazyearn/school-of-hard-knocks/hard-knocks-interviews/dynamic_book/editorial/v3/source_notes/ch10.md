## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

Every ownership claim now enters one six-field ledger:

1. current cash rights;
2. residual rights;
3. control rights;
4. information rights;
5. liquidity and transfer rights; and
6. duties and downside.

The ledger replaces overlapping employee-equity, partner-agreement, and
ownership-map checklists. It keeps exact instrument, percentage, payment,
priority, vesting, dilution, votes, inspection, tax, repurchase, transfer,
guarantees, labor, capital calls, liability, maintenance, and loss in one
inspectable bundle. Equal percentages can therefore describe radically
different freedom.

Knowledge, tools, documented systems, teams, and capital may extend useful
output beyond the owner's next hour. They do not establish a hierarchy of
human worth, erase enabling labor and institutions, or guarantee control of
time. Record 3 opens with Chapter 9's operating route and one smallest credible
claim whose economics, rights, authority, liquidity, obligations, and downside
are all visible.
