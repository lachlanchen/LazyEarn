## Focused Research Synthesis

Problem 01 protects the material floor, practical refusal capacity, fulfillment
per unit of life, and a governed stopping judgment. Problem 09 separates
kinship, ownership, employment, authority, provision, capability, and choice.
Focused readings of *Huainanzi*, *The Great Gatsby*, *Buddenbrooks*, and
*Father Goriot* remain invisible architecture for adaptation, status, dynasty,
and purchased affection. They do not enter as quotations, plot summaries,
name-dropping, or empirical evidence.

## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter now joins personal enough to intergenerational stewardship. A
longer horizon changes present consumption, leverage, care, governance, and
public responsibility, but does not require preserving one fortune, company,
surname, or assigned life. Provision should widen agency; it cannot purchase a
recipient's vocation, gratitude, identity, or intimacy.

Record 5 closes with one seven-question enough statement: material floor,
freedoms bought, legitimate claims, worthwhile work, risks worth bearing,
what and through whom wealth should pass, and what money may never purchase.
The ending returns the argument to an ordinary finite day, where accumulated
agency receives both a use and a boundary.
