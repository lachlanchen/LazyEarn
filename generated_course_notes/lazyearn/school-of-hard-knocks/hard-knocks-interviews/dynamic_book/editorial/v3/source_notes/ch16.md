## Focused Research Synthesis

The accepted growth-and-cash card supplies the attention-to-collection route,
the distinction between contribution and cash, and the rule that working
capital and delivery duties remain attached to growth. The ownership-and-control
card supplies the separation among current cash rights, residual economics,
control, information, liquidity, and downside.

The survival-before-return card contributes only an epistemic boundary here:
success, paper loss, and a creditor's cash demand are different observations.
Risk capacity, willingness, necessity, concentration policy, and forced-sale
judgment remain assigned to Chapter 18. Its focused statistical-mechanics
reading sharpens the distinction between an expected distribution and one
received path, but no physics metaphor or physical law appears in this chapter.

The curriculum disagreement audit limits the accounting material to the
grammar needed for a decision. The chapter does not become a miniature
accounting course, a valuation manual, a tax instruction, or personalized
investment advice.

## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The complete number address records entity and share, time, category, scope,
state, prior claims, source and confidence, and decision. It reconciles Record
3's operating route through collection and contribution to available company
cash, financing, and an authorized owner distribution.

The restaurant case demonstrates how to preserve a discrepancy without either
discarding the account or manufacturing a reconciliation. The portfolio and
restricted-stock cases keep value separate from realizable liquidity. The
industrial-property, Austin-stock, and New York-margin cases distinguish
financing exposure, marked loss, and a dated demand for cash before later
chapters judge the risk.

entry. A number cannot support admiration, borrowing, spending, or advice until
its category, timing, rights, conditions, and consequence of error are visible.
