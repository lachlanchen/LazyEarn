## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter completes the reciprocal exchange record opened in Chapter 7
rather than creating another sales checklist. It records the decision and
authorized stopping point; the claim, evidence rung, concern, disconfirmation,
and next test; actual commercial terms; and consent, safeguards, affected
people, and delivery obligations. Agreement, invoice, collected cash, accepted
delivery, repeat use, renewal, and healthy economics remain different events.

Urgency is restricted to a material fact whose source, date, consequence, and
alternatives can be stated. A seller may not shorten a window, hide an
extension, imply false scarcity, or enlarge fear. Aggregate patterns can
improve an offer, but a private dossier or repeated pressure on one person
cannot.
