## Focused Research Synthesis

Focused reading of Peter Drucker's *The Effective Executive* sharpened
observed time, contribution, consequential people decisions, organized
disagreement, and evidence-tested judgment. Marty Cagan's *Inspired*
helped distinguish an outcome-bearing team from a feature or task queue and
made authority boundaries and direct customer results visible. No named
corporate anecdote, fixed team size, slogan, or technology-specific practice
enters the reader text.

Selected *Analects* passages sharpened trust, reciprocity, conduct, and
harmony without enforced sameness. Selected *Han Feizi* passages exposed
information asymmetry, favoritism, named responsibility, and observable
consequence. Their ancient hierarchies are not combined into a management
formula: paternalism, ruler-centered suspicion, severe punishment, coercion,
and suppression of dissent are rejected.

The resulting synthesis treats trust and controls as complements. Trust makes
unwelcome truth speakable; explicit roles, reasons, records, review, and
challenge keep private influence from purchasing exemption. Bounded
responsibility joins outcome, authority, resources, limits, information,
support, review, and remedy. Transferring only the task is dumping work;
transferring authority without boundaries or support is abandonment.

## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter traces every consequential decision through promise, contribution,
power, and exposure. Durable shareholder return remains legitimate without
becoming automatic priority over safety, consent, contract, debt, supplier, or
public duties. Consultation expands with consequence, irreversibility, and
uncertainty; one role still closes the decision.

Record 3 receives one people-and-governance charter rather than separate
hiring, delegation, partnership, culture, and crisis worksheets. It records
promise and exposure, bounded responsibilities, economics, decisions and
truth, protection and support, and repair and continuity. Reversible evidence
precedes equity or wider consequence. Emergency authority has a trigger,
scope, communication duty, retained evidence, review, and expiry.
