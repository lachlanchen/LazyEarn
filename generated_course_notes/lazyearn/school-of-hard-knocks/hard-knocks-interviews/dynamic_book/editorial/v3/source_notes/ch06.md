## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The six-rung evidence ladder, smallest-faithful-test rule, and six-field
experiment contract are original synthesis from the interview evidence and the
accepted customer-evidence source card. Focused readings of conversation and
experiment literature supplied discipline, not reader-facing company stories
or branded doctrine. Each rung establishes only the role, terms, channel,
scope, and conditions actually reproduced:

1. specific past behavior;
2. existing workaround;
3. costly commitment;
4. bounded pilot;
5. viable delivery; and
6. repeated exchange.

The contract fixes claim, prediction, boundary, observation,
disconfirmation, and next decision before the result. It keeps consent,
privacy, law, safety, and professional duty outside the tradable test. It does
not let customer preference choose company strategy or let strategy override a
person's refusal.
