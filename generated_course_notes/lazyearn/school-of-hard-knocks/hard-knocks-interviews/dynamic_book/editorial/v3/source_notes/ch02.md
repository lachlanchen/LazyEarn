## Explanatory Boundary

The feasible-set and capacity/capability/access distinctions are an original
practical synthesis. They describe present options; they do not rank human
worth or claim to implement a formal welfare measure. Peace, public health,
care, infrastructure, law, currency, and institutions are included as
background conditions because they change which private actions are possible
and who bears their cost. No survey of economic schools or outside anecdote is
inserted into the narrative.

## Figure Decision

No candidate frame adds necessary evidence. Supercars, generic interviews,
