## Focused Verification

The United States Copyright Office's musician guidance and Circular 56A
confirm that a musical work and a sound recording are separate
copyright-protected works that may be owned and licensed separately:

- https://www.copyright.gov/engage/musicians/
- https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ56a.pdf

The United States Small Business Administration's current 7(a) page confirms
that the program exists while leaving eligibility, use, underwriting, terms,
and guarantees to current rules and the specific transaction:

- https://www.sba.gov/loans/7a-loans/

These sources support narrow distinctions and cautions only. The chapter does
not provide legal, tax, investment, lending, or acquisition advice.

## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The customer route is traced through physical, legal, contractual, and
relational layers before ownership is chosen. Bottleneck control is justified
only when it improves the promise after propagated failure, fairness,
substitution, and captivity are tested. Integration is treated as a
coordination choice with capital, fixed-cost, utilization, management, labor,
compliance, and concentration burdens, not as a prestige strategy.

The legal instrument populates the six ownership fields already opened in
Record 3: current cash, residual economics, control, information, liquidity
and transfer, and duties and downside. No competing rights ledger is opened.
The Arizona manufacturer and Dallas banker then resolve the unfinished
financing choice from Chapter 12 through one capital-fit record: constraint,
productive use, timing, rights and control, pace and reporting, failure,
alternatives, and stop or renegotiation evidence.

The chapter ends with one rights-and-critical-links update to the existing
route. Every material link receives function, bundle, validity and transfer,
dependency and failure, cost and duty, and choice and capital. Ownership,
contract, diversification, backup, redesign, and bounded acceptance remain
real alternatives.
