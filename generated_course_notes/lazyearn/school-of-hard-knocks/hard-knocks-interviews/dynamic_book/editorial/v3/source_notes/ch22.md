## Focused Research Synthesis

Problem 01 keeps money materially serious: freedom first reduces coercion,
stores time and refusal capacity, and protects needed goods from optional ruin.
Problem 09 keeps love, ownership, work, authority, belief, and personal
direction separate so shared purpose cannot conscript another person's life.
The literary and philosophical material reserved for the final enough and
transfer argument does not enter this chapter as quotation, name-dropping, or
decorative analogy.

## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter now moves through relief, recovery, chosen work, relationship,
play, learning, mastery, making, service, place, and chosen obligation.
Recovery is a protected good rather than a productivity investment. Chosen
work requires credible exit, rest, and revision; enthusiasm or continued
activity alone does not establish freedom.

Record 5 receives one freedom portfolio with current holdings, desired uses,
deliberate limits, calendar evidence, and review after changes in health,
household, work, wealth, place, or desire. It does not prescribe one balance,
family form, faith, career, public role, or post-exit destination.

## Figure Decision

No figure is accepted. Candidate yachts, private jets, interview portraits,
coffee routines, family stills, and generic leisure scenes would turn context
into decoration. The freedom portfolio is clearer as a textual practice.
