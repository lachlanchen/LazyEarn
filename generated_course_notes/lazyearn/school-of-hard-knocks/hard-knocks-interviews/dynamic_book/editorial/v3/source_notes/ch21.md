## Current Health Boundary

Official guidance was reconfirmed on 2026-08-26:

- [WHO, *Guidelines on mental health at work*](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240053052)
  covers organizational intervention, training, individual support, return to
  work, and participation in work.
- [WHO psychosocial-risk guidance](https://www.who.int/tools/occupational-hazards-in-health-sector/psycho-social-risks-mental-health)
  identifies workload, working time, control, support, and work organization as
  risk factors and prioritizes organizational reduction, schedule changes,
  communication, accommodation, breaks, and safe staffing.
- [SAMHSA trauma-informed approaches](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-approaches-programs)
  support safety, trust, collaboration, voice, choice, and avoidance of
  retraumatization.
- [NIMH psychotherapy guidance](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/psychotherapies)
  states that psychotherapy may be used alone or with medication and that a
  treatment plan depends on individual needs, medical circumstances, and
  professional guidance.

The reader text therefore changes preventable work conditions before asking a
person to endure them, protects privacy and choice, enables qualified care,
and gives no diagnosis, treatment order, or medication advice.

## Focused Research Synthesis

Focused readings of Frankl, *Zhuangzi*, and Tolstoy shape the reasoning without
appearing as authority theater. Meaning can orient responsibility but cannot
make avoidable suffering necessary. Mastery recognizes structure, preserves
the instrument, and leaves some opportunities unused. A publicly successful
life can conceal contrary bodily and relational evidence until change becomes
difficult. No quotation, historical trauma analogy, literary synopsis,
productivity fable, or illness-as-punishment claim enters the chapter.

## V3 Explanatory Synthesis

The chapter separates replaceable or reversible loss, repairable but
path-dependent loss, and non-substitutable or irrecoverable loss while
preserving money's real power to fund care, assistance, compensation, and
restitution. It holds personal agency beside wages, health, care, housing,
institutions, bargaining power, and luck rather than diagnosing character from
a balance.
