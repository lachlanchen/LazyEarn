# Chapter 11 V3 Acceptance Review

Status: accepted on 2026-08-26.

## Narrative Contract

- Tests whether expected work continues and an unexpected condition is noticed,
  bounded, escalated, supported, and reviewed while the founder is absent.
- Defines absence tolerance as designed stewardship, not abandonment or
  absentee ownership.
- Connects delegation to the existing evidence ladder, operating route,
  customer contact, and bounded permission to grow.
- Updates Record 3 rather than creating a separate systems workbook.

## V3 Work

- The duplicate lead-to-renewal diagram is removed. Chapter 9's route identifier
  is now the single test surface.
- Expected work carries invariant, reason, interface, owner, measure, and
  inspection; exceptions carry boundaries, escalation, expiring authority,
  support, stop rights, and review.
- Documentation explicitly preserves judgment and reasons while allowing the
  rule to change when evidence changes.
- Delegation begins with a reversible decision and expands only after observed
  outcome, customer response, resources, authority, context, and support justify
  the larger consequence.
- Renewal is connected to repeated-exchange evidence; a contract, switching
  cost, obscure cancellation, or inattention is not loyalty.
- Growth permission requires no hidden critical zero and a route capable of
  carrying the next demand increment without weakening safety, consent,
  quality, collection, or repair.
