## Narrative Contract

- Identifies who finances the delay between demand, delivery, acceptance,
  collection, payroll, maintenance, and repair.
- Separates accounting flows, balance-sheet stocks, and dated liquidity before
  allowing a growth decision.
- Makes growth conditional on demand, collected contribution, capacity, cash,
  duties, recoverability, and a survivable stress path.
- Carries the unresolved financing candidate into the later capital-fit
  decision rather than prescribing debt, equity, or bootstrap control here.

## V3 Work

- Revenue, gross profit, operating profit, cash, and balance-sheet capacity are
  kept in separate accounts; period flows cannot masquerade as cash at a date.
- A weekly cash calendar and peak-carry calculation replace intuition based on
  invoice dates or average cash-cycle days.
- Financing is compared through full cost, recourse, control, information,
  guarantees, and failure consequence. Every timing gap has a visible carrier.
- Future capability is separated from present output through a named
  capability, evidence date, carrying cost, and stop condition.
- The overlapping growth worksheets are consolidated into one Record 3
  permission: diagnosis, evidence, coherent change, carry, boundaries, stop,
  and next stage.
- Record 1 changes only when the step changes portable capability or the
  protected household floor.
