# Chapter 19 V3 Acceptance Review

Status: accepted on 2026-08-26.

## Narrative Contract

- Begins with volatile income and makes liquidity the time between interruption
  and an irreversible decision rather than wealth's destination.
- Separates controlled cash, dated receipts, convertible assets, conditional
  access, and illiquid value before assigning any reserve.
- Gives household continuity, operating runway, committed projects, and
  opportunity capital distinct jobs without prescribing four accounts or fixed
  percentages.
- Uses delayed commission income to test both financing and future capability,
  then places employee promises before optional distributions and distressed
  buying.
- Ends with one interruption map and hands losses too large to retain to
  Chapter 20's prevention, transfer, and containment analysis.

## V3 Work

- The opening now establishes two clocks: dated obligations and the time needed
  to understand and repair a shock.
- A credit facility is recorded with its counterparty, terms, conditions, and a
  no-new-credit path, but never counted as cash already controlled.
- Each reserve now has a job, owner, trigger, access path, duration,
  replenishment rule, and boundary. Transfers expose the displaced claim and
  require a restoration plan.
- Kevin Salmon's bridge becomes an evidence-bearing interval rather than a
  stamina story. Every review tests cash, traction, maximum season, health,
  relationships, skill, trust, judgment, and the condition that will extend,
  redesign, or end the arrangement.
- Cash, capability, and trust are kept distinct in recovery. Humane sequencing
  protects earned obligations and truthful communication before optional
  distributions or speculative projects.
- Opportunity capital exists only after household, operating, and committed
  claims survive stress. Excess cash also receives a release rule so prudence
  does not become indefinite avoidance.
- Record 4 now holds one eight-field interruption map; Record 1 changes only if
  the protected floor or capability plan changes explicitly.
