# Chapter 14 V3 Acceptance Review

Status: accepted on 2026-08-26.

## Narrative Contract

- Distinguishes the useful object from legal rights, economic claims,
  decision power, duties, and dependencies.
- Tests bottleneck ownership and integration by their effect on the customer
  promise rather than by asset count or founder prestige.
- Reconciles self-financing, debt, equity, and other capital through productive
  fit instead of declaring one route universally superior.
- Carries the operating system into succession by asking whether its rights,
  relationships, and reputation can survive a transfer.

## V3 Work

- The opening now distinguishes a musical work, sound recording, master,
  publishing interest, distribution, license, advance, recoupment, and
  transfer. Russ's catalog is correctly described as recordings rather than
  treating a master as the song itself.
- The value chain gains physical, legal, contractual, and relational layers.
  Centralization and integration are tested for propagated failure, fairness,
  substitution, rent extraction, utilization, management, and duty.
- Asset analysis returns to Chapter 10's six-field ownership claim. Oil,
  employee equity, founder control, entity separation, and acquisition terms
  populate that common ledger rather than opening another taxonomy.
- The Arizona manufacturer and Dallas banker become a genuine capital
  contradiction. Chapter 12's peak carry feeds one capital-fit record covering
  productive use, duration, surrendered rights, imposed pace, reporting,
  guarantees, exposed people, alternatives, and stop conditions.
- Record 3 receives one six-field rights-and-critical-links update. It does not
  duplicate the route, cash forecast, ownership claim, or growth decision.
