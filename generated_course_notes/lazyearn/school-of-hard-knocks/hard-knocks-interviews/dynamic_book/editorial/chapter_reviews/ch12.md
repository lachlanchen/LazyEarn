## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Revenue, gross profit, operating profit, cash, and balance-sheet capacity are
  separated before any growth decision.
- Peak funding is calculated from dated cash movements plus a named reserve;
  average cash-cycle days remain only a diagnostic.
- Customer, supplier, founder, worker, lender, factor, and investor burdens are
  made visible. Financing is compared by full cost, control, recourse, and
  failure consequence rather than headline rate alone.
- Emergency sales count as liquidity only when collected cash precedes the new
  delivery and recovery obligations.
- A professional accountant and owner financial literacy are complementary.
  Concealing information from a lawful lender is explicitly rejected against
  the crisis-candor evidence.
- The eight-step growth-carry worksheet connects demand, economics, peak cash,
  capacity, financing, stress cases, stop conditions, and weekly variance.
