## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Practice is expressed as a feedback cycle and qualified by instruction,
  health, safety, tools, team, opportunity, talent, and luck.
- Preference and comparative advantage are tested against reachable demand;
  college, short courses, and equipment are judged by capability, proof,
  permission, access, cost, and output rather than ideology.
- Employment can fund and teach ownership, but ownership without portable
  capital is exposure rather than independence.
- Saving cannot repair inadequate income. High-cost debt and permanent fixed
  spending are treated as claims on uncertain future labor, while a first
  reserve is assigned to decision time rather than advertised return.
- Household alignment requires consent from people carrying the downside; it
  does not instrumentalize a spouse or family.
- The seven-step plan produces a bounded experiment, not a wealth promise.
