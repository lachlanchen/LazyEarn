# Chapter 21 Review

## Decision

Accepted for the working manuscript. The chapter makes bodily, relational,
ethical, institutional, and third-party consequences part of the economics of
ambition. It preserves demanding work and chosen sacrifice without treating
injury, isolation, unpaid claims, or damaged relationships as proof of
seriousness.

## Narrative Role

- Opens Part V with health as a non-substitutable constraint rather than a
  sentimental counterpoint to wealth.
- Distinguishes adaptive, bounded difficulty from chronic or non-consensual
  damage.
- Makes creditors, workers, customers, family, and communities visible in the
  downside distribution of a founder's bet.
- Uses public service to test purpose against conflict, criticism, information
  asymmetry, oversight, and term boundaries.
- Treats changed friendship and family dynamics without prescribing secrecy
  or unlimited rescue.
- Preserves the trauma discussion while refusing amateur diagnosis or changes
  to prescribed care.
- Produces a seven-account cost review and explicit stop rules, then turns to
  Chapter 22's positive use of freedom.

## Health Boundary

The health and trauma boundary was checked against official U.S. sources on
2026-08-25:

- [SAMHSA trauma-informed care](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-care)
  recognizes physically or emotionally harmful or threatening experiences and
  possible effects across functioning and well-being.
- [SAMHSA's trauma-informed approach](https://www.samhsa.gov/mental-health/trauma-violence/trauma-informed-approaches-programs)
  emphasizes safety, trust, peer support, collaboration, voice, choice, and
  avoiding retraumatization.
- [NIMH PTSD guidance](https://www.nimh.nih.gov/health/topics/post-traumatic-stress-disorder-ptsd)
  identifies psychotherapy, medication, or a combination as treatment routes
  requiring individual evaluation by experienced professionals.
- [CDC stress guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/mental-health/living-with/index.html)
  distinguishes ordinary stress from unresolved long-term stress and directs
  people who struggle to cope toward support.

The chapter therefore rejects White's medication-versus-support binary,
preserves only the need to recognize non-visible harm, and prohibits diagnosis
or treatment changes by a founder, coach, manager, relative, or book.

## Contradictions Resolved

- Pressure rehearsal and disciplined bodily care can coexist; injury is not a
  credential.
- Chosen intensity can be meaningful while “all in” can also conceal escape
  from ordinary obligations.
- Persistence after bankruptcy can be admirable while creditors' losses and
  family damage remain real rather than becoming props in a comeback story.
- Purpose can strengthen difficult service while also rationalizing conflicts,
  secrecy, weak oversight, and uncompensated burdens.
- Financial change can strain relationships without making the richer person
  or the old relationship automatically wrong.
- Quality time matters while lost quantity cannot be bought back.
- Small routines can restore evidence of agency without replacing medical,
  mental-health, legal, or financial help.
- Imbalance can be chosen for a season while a season without a dated end is an
  unexamined life structure.
