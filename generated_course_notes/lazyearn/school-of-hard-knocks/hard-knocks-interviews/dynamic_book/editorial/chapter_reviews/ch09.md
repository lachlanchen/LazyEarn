  help, focus, and product-before-distribution claim. It is paired with
  Lubetzky's access case as a complement rather than a contradiction.

  repeated customer choice, product, execution, and team. Its customer-as-

- Source 92's opening manufacturing account supports the pandemic conversion

  reported windfall and adds quality, compliance, procurement, and public-duty

- Source 23 recurs only through the already established awareness-to-trust
  sequence; the chapter extends it through delivery, return, referral, and
  recovery.
- No interview wording is presented as a direct quotation.

## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Product truth and distribution access are treated as complements. Neither
  can rescue the absence of the other.
- Audience size is separated from qualified attention, trust, transaction,
  return, and referral. Platform attention is rented rather than owned.
- Brand recognition is separated from reputation; the latter is the remembered
  pattern of delivery and repair after a promise.
- Relationships can carry information through earned trust, but generosity is
  not an invoice for access and inherited networks are not evidence of superior
  merit.
- Route contribution includes acquisition, variable delivery, service,
  recovery, retention probability, and a defensible time horizon rather than
  celebrating gross first-purchase revenue.
- The nine-step distribution map gives the reader one route to inspect and one
  bounded repair to test.

## Figure Decision

- Visual inventories for all eleven primary sources contain only generic
  interview shots, cars, logos, exteriors, promotional overlays, or repeated
  poses. None provides object, process, setting, or documentary evidence that
  materially deepens the argument. Chapter 9 therefore contains no figure.
