## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Resourcefulness converts available help into action but cannot make access
  equal or remove licensing, care, health, legal, discrimination, or safety
  constraints.
- Skill survives loss only as a bundle with proof, tools, trust, health,
  permission, demand, and runway.
- Luck changes the return to effort without making later decisions irrelevant.
- Credit is treated as a future claim, not as free starting capital; rental and
  paid-network claims retain their operating, legal, and selection risks.
- The opportunity inventory separates available, buildable,
  counterparty-dependent, and structurally blocked options and requires a loss
  budget and review date.
