## Contradiction And Usefulness

- A desired life must coexist with competence and reachable demand; passion is
  neither proof of market fit nor a duty to commercialize every vocation.
- Stable work can fund care or a noncommercial purpose and is not treated as
  cowardice. Indefinite postponement remains a cost that must be reviewed.
- Health, relationship, and material boundaries make ambition survivable and
  require consent from people carrying the downside.
- Perseverance is bounded by evidence, duration, recovery, and stopping rules.
  Learning is not presented as compensation for ruin or harm to others.
- The five-year test prices ordinary process rather than launch-day spectacle.
  The life-first sheet remains revisable when the life genuinely changes.
