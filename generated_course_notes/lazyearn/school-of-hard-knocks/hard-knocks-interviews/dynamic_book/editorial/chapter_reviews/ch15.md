## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Saleability is a stress test for continuity, not the company's mission.
  Continued ownership, family or management succession, employee ownership,
  partial transfer, partnership, sale, and orderly closure remain legitimate
  alternatives.
- Continuity, succession, legal transfer, and sale are kept distinct. A company
  can achieve one without the others.
- Procedures preserve steps; succession also requires evidence, limits,
  exceptions, permissions, unresolved risks, history, judgment, and authority.
- Enterprise value is separated from seller proceeds and owner-specific cash.
  Contingencies, preferences, debt, liabilities, costs, working capital,
  holdbacks, tax, and later performance can change the result.
- Personal reputation can travel across companies and simultaneously create
  platform, truth, health, departure, and systemic key-person risk.
- Price is compared with customer, worker, privacy, safety, environmental,
  professional, supplier, lender, community, and mission obligations. Transfer
  does not cleanse an asset's history.
- The ten-point transferability review is the practical payoff and creates a
  twelve-month rehearsal even when no transaction is planned.
