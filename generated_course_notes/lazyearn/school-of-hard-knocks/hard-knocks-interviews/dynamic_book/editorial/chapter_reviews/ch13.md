## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Hiring for weakness is converted into a six-question contribution brief;
  hiring smarter does not mean reproducing the founder or erasing worker agency.
- Partnership trust is earned through reversible work and made survivable by
  explicit economics, information rights, capital duties, decision domains,
  deadlock, incapacity, departure, transfer, and sale terms.
- Horizontal collaboration is joined to one accountable decision owner and a
  bounded, expiring emergency authority.
- Psychological safety includes challenge and evidence rather than comfort or
  consensus. High revenue cannot purchase immunity from customer, worker,
  safety, or integrity obligations.
- Pay, credit, workload, information, voice, field safety, and non-retaliation
  are treated as governance mechanisms. Leadership steadiness is paired with
  calibrated disclosure and competent private support rather than concealment.
- The ten-step people-and-governance charter is the chapter's practical payoff
  and is stress-tested against cash, product, harassment, partner, illness, and
  integrity cases.
