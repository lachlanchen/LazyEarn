## Contradiction And Usefulness

- An asset carries debt only through collected cash, supported by reserves and
  borrower response; scheduled rent and collateral are insufficient.
- The lender bears credit risk while the borrower, equity providers,
  guarantors, workers, residents, customers, and community may bear different
  losses.
- NOI is kept distinct from owner cash after debt, capital work, tax, and other
  required spending.
- Owner-occupied rent offsets housing only after vacancy, collection, operating
  cost, law, and financing are included.
- Seller finance changes the lender but preserves payment, collateral, default,
  diligence, and resident obligations.
- A cash-out refinance releases borrowed cash and can reduce the remaining
  equity cushion.
- A good asset can still fail at rate reset, maturity, or refinancing.
- The nine-step leverage survival test is the practical payoff and rejects
  appreciation or an uncommitted future loan as the sole repair.
