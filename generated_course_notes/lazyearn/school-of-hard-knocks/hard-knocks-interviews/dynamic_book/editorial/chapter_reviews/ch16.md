## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Every number receives a category, owner, period, state, source, and date
  before it supports a decision.
- Revenue, ARR, operating profit, change in cash, company reserves, owner pay,
  and owner liquidity remain separate.
- An eighteen-month forecast is explicitly distinguished from eighteen months
  of liquid runway.
- The restaurant discrepancy is retained as evidence uncertainty rather than
  corrected toward the most dramatic claim.
- Public and private marks remain distinct from realized proceeds; restrictions,
  senior claims, dilution, tax, concentration, and transaction friction remain
  visible.
- High income is not treated as durable wealth, while restrained consumption is
  not treated as proof of wealth.
- The eight-field number card is the practical payoff and provides the input
  Chapter 17 needs to evaluate leverage.
