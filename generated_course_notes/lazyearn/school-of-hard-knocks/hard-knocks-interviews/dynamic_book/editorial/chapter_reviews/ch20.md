# Chapter 20 Review

## Decision

Accepted for the working manuscript. The chapter preserves the corpus's tax,
entity, documentation, leverage, insurance, contract, and adviser mechanisms
without presenting an interview claim as current legal permission. It gives
the reader one risk-shell method that begins with the loss rather than a
promoted product.

## Narrative Role

- Opens with the incompatible-looking reported tax outcomes of Carlton Dennis,
  Richard Harpin, and a UAE-resident family-business operator.
- Separates cash spent, current tax reduction, timing, basis, economic value,
  and reinvestment performance.
- Uses the visible short-term-rental board to distinguish arithmetic from
  eligibility.
- Makes entity choice an operating and governance decision rather than a force
  field or automatic tax switch.
- Places contracts, insurance, disclosure, and adviser conflicts around
  prevention and reserves without claiming that risk can be erased.
- Produces an eight-line risk-shell review and turns Part IV toward the finite
  life that capital is meant to protect.

## Current-Law Boundary

Tax and legal rules are jurisdiction-, date-, entity-, and fact-dependent. The
chapter's general qualifications were checked against official U.S. sources on
2026-08-25; readers are directed to current, transaction-specific review:

- [IRS Publication 334](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p334): ordinary and
  necessary business expenses, separation of business and personal use, and
  capitalization of some costs.
- [IRS S corporation compensation guidance](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporation-compensation-and-medical-insurance-issues):
  reasonable compensation before non-wage distributions and possible
  reclassification.
- [IRS Publication 946](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-publication-946):
  depreciation as cost recovery for qualifying business or income-producing
  property.
- [IRS private-foundation overview](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/charitable-organizations/private-foundations)
  and [self-dealing guidance](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/private-foundations/acts-of-self-dealing-by-private-foundation):
  exempt-purpose, distribution, holdings, investment, expenditure, and
  self-dealing restrictions.

The chapter intentionally omits current percentages, universal thresholds,
and prospective-law claims. It does not independently validate the yacht audit
or apply short-term-rental, vehicle, foundation, residence, or home-rental
rules to a reader.

## Contradictions Resolved

- A lower tax bill can preserve cash while the deductible purchase still costs
  money and may lose economic value.
- Depreciation can change taxable timing without proving that the asset or the
  reinvestment is sound.
- Fast implementation can preserve an available election while speed without
  complete facts magnifies error.
- A separate entity can allocate rights and contain some liabilities while
  guarantees, conduct, statutory duties, and poor separation can preserve
  personal exposure.
- Legal residence planning and civic judgment about public contribution are
  treated as distinct questions.
- Insurance can transfer a bounded peril while exclusions, limits, conditions,
  counterparty strength, and seller incentives leave residual risk.
- Contracts clarify promises but cannot erase non-waivable duties or guarantee
  that an insolvent counterparty can perform an indemnity.
