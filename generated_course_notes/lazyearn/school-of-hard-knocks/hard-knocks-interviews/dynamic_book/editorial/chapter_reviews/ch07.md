## Contradiction And Usefulness

- The value equation includes expected gain, avoided loss, time, option value,
  implementation burden, and downside, with explicit instructions against
  double counting and false precision.
- The accelerated-launch example distinguishes revenue from contribution,
  probability, ramp, cannibalization, switching cost, and standard of care.
- Strategic fit can produce buyer-specific value without making acquisition
  prices a benchmark for ordinary customers or dismissing weak revenue evidence.
- Price, budget, and willingness to pay remain distinct. Urgency increases both
  potential value and the duty to protect informed choice.
- The one-page value brief names buyer, baseline, outcome, evidence, seller
  floor, price range, duties, safeguards, and review conditions.
