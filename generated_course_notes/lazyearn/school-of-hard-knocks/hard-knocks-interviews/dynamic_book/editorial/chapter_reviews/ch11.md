## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Absence tolerance is defined as bounded stewardship, not founder neglect.
- Documentation is required where it preserves outcomes, safety, authority, or
  judgment; administrative motion and universal scripting are rejected.
- Delegation requires outcome, authority, resources, standards, visibility, and
  review. It does not make worker time cheap or relocate unsupported risk.
- Cohort retention and visible contribution are planning records rather than
  proof of loyalty. Refunds, failed collection, continuing service cost,
  voluntary exit, and recovery cost remain visible.
- Repeatability is useful only where variation harms the promise. A narrow
  product simplifies operations while concentrating product and input risk.
- The founder-dependence audit covers the complete customer-and-cash route,
  refuses to average away a critical zero, and uses announced staged absences
  rather than unsafe surprise tests.
