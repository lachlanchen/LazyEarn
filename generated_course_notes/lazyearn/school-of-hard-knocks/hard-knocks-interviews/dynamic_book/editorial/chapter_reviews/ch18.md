# Chapter 18 Review

## Decision

Accepted for the working manuscript. The chapter turns conflicting investment
testimony into a portfolio-policy method without recommending an asset,
promising a return, or treating a selected winner as a general result.

## Narrative Role

- Opens with the slow left side of compounding, then makes survival the
  condition for receiving a long-horizon result.
- Reconciles founder concentration with later preservation rather than
  declaring either concentration or diversification universally correct.
- Corrects the Dubai compounding illustration and distinguishes fees, tax,
  turnover, financing, vacancy, dilution, and idle cash.
- Tests the recurring-cash “river” against operating and leverage risk.
- Ends with a reader-usable portfolio policy and hands the reserve problem to
  Chapter 19.

## Contradictions Resolved

- Concentration may be useful during informed creation and dangerous during
  preservation.
- Diversification reduces specific dependence but cannot remove market,
  liquidity, inflation, institutional, or household risk.
- Cash can drag long-run return while preserving the ability to wait.
- Recurring cash flow may support freedom while remaining operationally
  fragile.
- Tax deferral and refinancing can preserve or release capital without making
  a weak asset economically sound.
- Fixed allocation rules conflict because obligations, horizons, knowledge,
  and loss capacity differ.
