## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Customers reveal local burden and broken promises without necessarily
  knowing the best design. Listening is synthesis, not voting or obedience.
- Stated preference is separated from past behavior, costly response, pilot
  commitment, payment, use, renewal, and referral. No rung proves a market.
- Complaints are paired with positive evidence so repair does not destroy the
  reason loyal customers stay. Review evidence remains a biased sample.
- A small test is bounded by safety, privacy, law, and standard of care.
- Trend claims are separated from durable constraints, forced change, specific
  buyer burden, and reachable first use; disconfirming evidence is named before
  investment grows.
- The learning record preserves contrary evidence and decisions without
  retaining unnecessary personal or confidential material.
