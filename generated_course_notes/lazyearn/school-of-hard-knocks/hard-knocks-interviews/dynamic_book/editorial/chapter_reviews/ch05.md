## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Human pain, social benefit, price, and captured income are kept distinct.
  The user, affected person, decision-maker, and payer may be different people.
- A large market does not establish fit, reach, trust, or an obtainable share.
  A narrow reachable decision path is more useful than inflated TAM arithmetic.
- Technology, novelty, and founder enthusiasm do not substitute for buyer
  evidence. Market knowledge does not excuse technical or safety failure.
- Urgency raises both willingness to pay and exploitation risk. The practical
  problem brief therefore includes an ethical limit, risk bearer, and proof of
  reduced burden.
- The chapter's cost identity is an accounting aid, not false precision; pain,
  dignity, anxiety, and ecological damage need not be converted into dollars.
