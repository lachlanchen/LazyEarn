## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Ownership is a bundle of residual rights and duties, not a status word or an
  automatic source of wealth. It can require capital, absorb loss, and remain
  illiquid or worthless.
- Salary is retained as a rational exchange for stability, benefits,
  diversification, legal protection, and paid learning. Minority ownership can
  add upside while supplying little control.
- Current cash compensation is separated from uncertain claim value and owner
  contributions or losses in a two-line classification aid.
- Personal reputation can improve bargaining power, but a human life is not a
  permanently available commercial asset.
- Complementary ownership is tested against capital, flexibility, specialist
  advantage, professional conflict, customer duty, and public obligation.
- Operator equity is assessed through labor, capital, information, governance,
  dilution, distribution, transfer, deadlock, and downside rather than an
  unsupported alignment slogan.
- The seven-step ownership map asks for the next credible claim, not a leap
  from employment into concentrated founder risk.
