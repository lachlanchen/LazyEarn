## Contradiction And Usefulness

- Persistence moves to another qualified buyer or an improved offer; it does
  not wear down one person's boundary.
- Concerns are classified as fit, evidence, understanding, trust, economics,
  decision process, or refusal. Only the first six invite consensual inquiry.
- Truth is part of delivered value. Benefits, material limits, uncertainty,
  conflicts, alternatives, and reasons not to recommend are visible.
- The humane sales review records decision criteria, evidence, concern,
  response, outcome, next learning, and a stopping point without inventing a
  reason for refusal or creating a private dossier.
- Rejection is ordinary evidence; threats, harassment, discrimination, and
  violence require employer protection and an immediate safe exit.
