# How You Got Rich Book Memory

## Governing Idea

A well-lived life is the end. Wealth is infrastructure: it can buy time,
resilience, choice, care, and room to act, but it cannot decide what deserves a
life. Leadership is stewardship of the people and systems that make durable
business, freedom, and happiness possible.

## Evidence Hierarchy

1. Timestamped interview transcript and matching video.
2. Per-interview analysis, narrative map, and durable memory fragment.
3. Cross-interview recurrence and contradiction.
4. Relevant finance, economics, entrepreneurship, ethics, communication, and
   happiness references from LazyEarn and LazyLearn.
5. Editorial interpretation, clearly separated from reported experience.

External references may clarify a mechanism but may not overwrite the primary
interview evidence or import unsupported facts into a speaker's story.

## Voice

- One lucid, humane authorial intelligence; never a fake interviewee.
- Concrete story before abstraction when a good source scene exists.
- Claims proportional to evidence; practical without promising wealth.
- Economical paragraphs with varied rhythm and few ornamental headings.
- No production language, source-processing narration, or mechanical recap.

## Persistent Tensions

- Ownership creates upside and responsibility.
- Concentration can build a fortune; diversification can preserve one.
- Debt can accelerate productive assets or destroy optionality.
- Persistence raises encounter probability but does not guarantee success.
- Visibility creates distribution and exposure.
- Ambition can enlarge agency while consuming the life it was meant to free.
- Advice from winners is evidence, not proof that luck and starting conditions
  did not matter.

## Acceptance Rule

No chapter is accepted until its sources are traceable, its contradictions are
visible, its prose reads naturally in sequence, and its practical guidance
states the relevant risks and limits.
