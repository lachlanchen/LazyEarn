## Lecture Arc
The lecture opens with a forecast question about the next five to ten years in technology. Instead of giving a clean list of three disconnected trends, the speaker immediately pivots to a stronger claim: part of the future has already arrived, and the pandemic accelerated it.

From there he names remote work, then broadens the frame from where people work to how they work together. That pivot matters, because collaboration becomes the deeper mechanism and remote work becomes the visible consequence.

The next transition is historical and explanatory. He contrasts the past, when the necessary tools were not available, with the present, when fast internet access and collaborative software make distributed work practical.

He then grounds that mechanism in ordinary products, moving from abstract infrastructure to Zoom, Slack, and even email. The close is a recap: collaboration is the future of tech work, and remote work follows from that collaborative operating environment.
