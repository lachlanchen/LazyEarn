   Move from portfolio scale to operating craft: 90 percent of success as showing up, clockwork reliability, customer details in restaurants, handwritten notes, and industrial real estate sales through listening. The mechanism is trust created through repeated behavior, not abstract charisma.

6. **Marketing, Relationships, And Brand Formation**  
   Connect the private equity speaker’s “marketing” claim to the later renewable-energy marketer’s emphasis on networking outside social platforms and the champagne founder’s relationships-plus-product thesis. Keep the contrast: marketing can distribute attention, but durable premium value also requires quality, margin knowledge, and positioning.

7. **Real Estate, Tax Caveats, And Asset Structure**  
   Treat the entity, lease-to-yourself, REIT, tax-break, and industrial real estate passages as claims requiring caution. The chapter should preserve them as interview claims, then explain the mechanism at a high level without giving legal or tax advice.

8. **Closing Synthesis: Wealth As Repeated Leverage**  
   End by tying together the repeated levers: disciplined saving, ownership, asymmetric bets, trusted relationships, operational reliability, sales listening, research, and margin control. The final tone should be practical and source-conscious: the interviews do not prove a formula, but they reveal recurring business patterns.
