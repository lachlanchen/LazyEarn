## Derivation Steps

1. Business selection mechanism:
   1. Start with five possible online businesses.
   2. Recognize that each requires repeated work.
   3. Choose the one with the strongest personal interest.
   4. Use interest as the endurance filter for skill-building.

2. Personal brand monetization:
   1. Create recurring content around a topic or identity.
   2. Build audience attention and trust.
   3. Monetize through ad revenue and brand deals.
   4. Use audience trust to launch adjacent businesses.

3. Creator-for-brand bridge:
   1. A personal brand may not support full-time income early.
   2. Companies still need daily content.
   3. Sell content creation services to those companies.
   4. Keep building the personal brand in parallel.

4. Video editing opportunity:
   1. Creators and companies produce long-form or raw content.
   2. They need clips adapted for TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube.
   3. They often lack time to do the repurposing.
   4. The editor sells saved time and platform-ready output.

5. E-commerce constraint:
   1. A product brand can scale beyond the founder.
   2. But customer acquisition cannot rely only on launch attention.
   3. Marketing skill is needed throughout the year.
   4. Startup capital is needed for product and promotion.

6. Social media marketing mechanism:
   1. A local business may have good food, service, or products.
   2. It may not be showing those products effectively online.
   3. The marketer offers monthly management.
   4. If organic reach is near zero, ads become part of solving the problem.

7. Online course mechanism:
   1. Start with expertise or proven success.
   2. Package the process as an A-to-Z framework.
   3. Sell it as an additional income stream.
   4. Use Udemy or Skillshare to reach beyond existing followers.
