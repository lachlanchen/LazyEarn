## Definitions And Objects
- \(C\): gross commission on a closed deal.
- \(C_{\mathrm{agent}}\): agent’s retained commission under a given split.
- \(C_{\mathrm{brokerage}}\): brokerage’s retained commission under a given split.
- \(Y_{\mathrm{req}}\): years-of-experience requirement for broker licensing.
- \(P_{\mathrm{req}}\): point threshold in the broker-licensing system.
- Broker: the supervisory licensed party; in the lecture this includes being “responsible,” “on the hook,” training agents, and supporting them.
- Agent: a licensed salesperson who cannot operate independently without a broker.
- Split structure: the percentage allocation of gross commission between agent and brokerage.
- \(V_{\mathrm{contract}}\): contract price at which the property is put under agreement.
- \(V_{\mathrm{exit}}\): later buyer’s price or effective exit price used to generate the wholesale spread.
- \(S\): gross spread between exit and contract prices.
- \(E_{\mathrm{earnest+option}}\): combined earnest money and option money used to secure contract control.
- Wholesale deal: in the lecture’s usage, putting a property under contract, controlling that contract, and then transferring or monetizing that control when another buyer is found.
- \(G\): gross fee headline from the wholesale transaction.
- \(n_p\): number of partners sharing the deal; here \(n_p = 3\).
- \(G_{\mathrm{per\ partner}}\): rough equal split per partner before tax.
- \(R_{\mathrm{shock}}\): ratio comparing first real-estate commission to prior wage-scale paycheck.
- “Traction”: the operating framework named by the speaker for dividing organizational roles into implementer and visionary.
- Implementer: the operator handling profitability, hiring, firing, and execution.
- Visionary: the role handling networking, relationship-building, and new ideas.
- \(R_q\): quarterly revenue of the cannabis company, as spoken.
- \(M\): market valuation of the cannabis company, as spoken.
- \(\Delta V_{\mathrm{day}}\): daily change in the value of the cannabis-stock position, as spoken.

## Derivation Steps

1. Take the spoken contract price \(V_{\mathrm{contract}} \approx 2.0\,\mathrm{M}\).
2. Take the spoken exit offer \(V_{\mathrm{exit}} \approx 2.7\,\mathrm{M}\).
3. Compute the spread \(S = V_{\mathrm{exit}} - V_{\mathrm{contract}}\).
4. Infer \(S \approx 0.7\,\mathrm{M}\), matching the repeated “700K” scale.
5. Keep the separate 3% commission claim distinct unless audio review clarifies whether it is included in or added to the spread.
6. Split the gross figure across three partners.
7. Then reduce further by taxes; do not equate gross fee with take-home.

Commission split comparison
1. Let \(C\) denote gross commission.
2. Under the current split, assign \(0.9C\) to the agent and \(0.1C\) to the brokerage.
3. Under the earlier split, assign \(0.5C\) to the agent and \(0.5C\) to the brokerage.
4. Compare agent economics via \(0.9C/0.5C = 1.8\).
5. Use this only as a gross-share comparison, not as a full profitability model.

Broker-licensing threshold logic

2. Record the old requirement as 2 years plus sales experience.
3. Record the newer requirement as 4 years plus transaction points.
4. Encode the point system as \(P_{\mathrm{req}} \approx 900\).
5. Add class-credit requirements.
6. Note that the speaker says college plus master’s degree satisfied those education credits.

## Notation Choices
- Use \(C\) for gross commission throughout.
- Use subscripts rather than prose labels when possible:
  \(C_{\mathrm{agent}}, C_{\mathrm{brokerage}}, Y_{\mathrm{req}}, P_{\mathrm{req}}, V_{\mathrm{contract}}, V_{\mathrm{exit}}\).
- Use \(\mathrm{M}\) for millions and \(\mathrm{k}\) for thousands in compact working equations.
- Use \(\approx\) rather than \(=\) for nearly all spoken deal numbers.
- Use \(S\) only for gross wholesale spread; do not reuse \(S\) for shares or splits.
- Use \(E_{\mathrm{earnest+option}}\) as a single combined variable, because the transcript combines earnest money and option money into one spoken figure.
- Use \(n_p=3\) for the partner count when splitting the wholesale fee.
- Use \(R_{\mathrm{shock}}\) for motivational income ratio and keep it separate from revenue notation.
- Use \(R_q\) for quarterly revenue and \(M\) for market value in the cannabis-stock segment.
- Keep “implementer” and “visionary” as quoted role labels, not algebraic symbols.
- Avoid legal notation implying statutory precision; prefer compact threshold notation with explicit caution.
