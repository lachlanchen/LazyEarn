## Definitions And Objects

- \(C\): unit cost paid to a manufacturer or supplier.
- \(P\): sale price charged to the customer.
- \(\pi\): gross profit per unit before overhead, ads, refunds, platform fees, shipping, taxes, or labor.
- \(\mathrm{ROI}\): simple unit-level return relative to the cost basis.
- \(m\): gross margin as a fraction of sale price.
- \(r\): affiliate commission rate.
- \(S_{\mathrm{referred}}\): sales attributed to the creator or affiliate.
- \(A\): brand ambassador or paid advertisement campaign payout.

## Derivation Steps

1. E-commerce unit economics:
   1. Choose a product, such as the transcript’s t-shirt example.
   2. Source it at a unit cost \(C\), roughly \(5\), \(10\), or \(15\) dollars.
   3. Sell it at a price \(P\), roughly \(20\), \(25\), or \(30\) dollars.
   4. Compute gross profit: \(\pi = P-C\).
   5. If needed, compute simple ROI: \(\mathrm{ROI}=(P-C)/C\).
   6. State clearly that this excludes marketing cost, fulfillment, platform fees, and returns.

   4. Use distribution to turn unit margin into repeated sales.
