## Definitions And Objects

- \(\mathrm{Observer}\): the past operating as thought, memory, experience, image, hurt, hope, tradition, and conditioned response.

- \(\mathrm{Known}\): accumulated knowledge, memory, experience, tradition, image, and the structure of the past.

- \(\mathrm{Thought}\): response of the past; not free and not new.

- \(\mathrm{I}\): the observer understood as the whole structure of what has been.

- \(\mathrm{Knowledge}_{\mathrm{function}}\): necessary practical knowledge used in mechanical function, language, travel, writing, ordinary action.

- \(\mathrm{Knowledge}_{\mathrm{relationship}}\): knowledge as image, memory, past experience, and observer operating between human beings.

- \(\mathrm{Creative\ Action}\): action not born from modified repetition or novelty; depends on freedom from the known.

- \(\mathrm{Novelty}\): something new-looking but still tied to the past, success, eccentricity, attention, or modified repetition.

- \(\mathrm{Relationship}\): the central field of life; not merely social contact, but the place where division or freedom is tested.

- \(\mathrm{Freedom}\): not chaos and not rejection of knowledge; freedom from the observer, linked to order and virtue.

- \(\mathrm{Attention}\): gathered energy; later linked with listening and observation without the observer.

- \(\mathrm{Regeneration}\): possible transformation not produced by influence, propaganda, threat, punishment, or reward.

## Derivation Steps

1. Observer as past:
   1. Thought is the response of past knowledge, experience, and memory.
   2. The observer is put together by thought.
   3. Therefore the observer is the past.
   4. When the observer observes, it observes through memory, hurt, hope, and conditioning.
   5. Therefore observation through the observer is already divided.

2. Division to conflict:
   1. The observer looks from a conditioned background.
   2. That background separates observer and observed.
   3. Separation becomes division: me/you, us/them, believer/nonbeliever, nation/nation.
   4. Where there is division, there is conflict.
   5. Conflict extends into violence.

3. Freedom from the known:
   1. The known repeats the past.
   2. Repetition can be modified and still remain repetition.
   3. Modified repetition is not creative action.
   4. Creative action requires freedom from the known.
   5. Freedom from the known is not denial of knowledge.
   6. It is understanding the known.
   7. Understanding brings intelligence, which is the essence of freedom.

4. Functional knowledge versus relational knowledge:
   1. Knowledge is necessary for ordinary function.
   2. Examples: going home, speaking English, writing a letter.
   3. The same knowledge, when used in relationship as image and memory, creates a barrier.
   4. The barrier divides you and me.
   5. Division in relationship produces conflict.
   6. Therefore knowledge has a proper place in function but not as observer in relationship.

5. Observer in relationship:
   1. Human life is relationship.
   2. The observer in relationship is past knowledge/image.
   3. The observer is the factor of division.
   4. Division means conflict.
   5. Therefore where the observer enters, actual relationship is absent.

6. Freedom, order, virtue:
   1. Freedom in relationship is not disorder.
   2. Freedom must be orderly.
   3. Order means virtue.
   4. Virtue is living, creative action, not repetition.
   5. Therefore freedom, order, and creative action belong together.

7. Fragment and whole:
   1. The existing mind is fragmented.
   2. The fragment tries to become the whole.
   3. There is no passage from fragment to whole.
   4. Gradual progress within fragmentation is still fragmentation.
   5. Modification of disorder is more disorder.

8. Regeneration:
   1. The human mind has evolved in contradiction, duality, and division.
   2. Change produced by reward, punishment, influence, or propaganda is not real change.
   3. The mind must observe the fact of division.
   4. If the observer observes it, conflict continues.
   5. Observation without the observer is the possible opening for regeneration.
