## Definitions And Objects

- `communication`: Not merely verbal exchange; includes listening, sharing, thinking together, creating together, and non-verbal communion.

- `listening`: Attention without acceptance, denial, comparison, or judgment.

- `attention`: Complete attention to the issue, problem, fact, crisis, or success; not concentration as a technique.

- `insight`: Non-verbal seeing that arises with real listening; not delayed conclusion.

- `communion`: Shared seriousness, passion, and attention around the same problem.

- `seriousness`: Intent, urge, total responsibility, action, and doing; not pain, grimness, entertainment, or wanting something.

- `responsibility`: To respond adequately, completely, and totally to challenge.

- `challenge` / `crisis`: Always new; if treated through the past, it is translated rather than met.

- `past`: The accumulated conclusions, plans, prejudices, fears, pleasures, routines, traditions, and conditioning through which the observer responds.

- `observer`: The self as past, conclusion, and separative translation.

- `observed`: The problem, chaos, society, crisis, or world that is mistakenly treated as outside the observer.

- `problem` / `fact`: What must be observed and listened to without bringing an answer to it.

- `negation`: Actual understanding and ending of what is false or immoral; not violent rejection, suppression, sacrifice, or verbal denial.

- `success`: Includes worldly achievement, money, position, authority, and also so-called spiritual achievement.

- `self-denial`: Ambiguous term; usable only if redefined away from sacrifice, pain, and punishment.

- `diligence`: Complete attention to the fact of success.
