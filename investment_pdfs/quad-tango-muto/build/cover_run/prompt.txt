Design a polished front-cover image for a literary-philosophical book titled "Quad Tango Muto".

Treat the title as design context only. Do not render any visible words, letters, numbers, logos, or publisher marks inside the artwork.

Core meaning to express:
- a four-part structure
- dance-like motion and tension
- touch causing transformation
- quiet power rather than spectacle
- a serious bookstore-ready literary cover, not a romance poster or nightclub image

Visual direction:
A refined editorial composition built around four presences or four movement traces arranged in rotational tension around a changing center. The image should suggest that contact alters matter: surfaces shift, lines bend, fragments realign, or light changes state where forms meet. The scene should feel like choreography, transformation, pressure, and aftermath all at once.

Keep it metaphorical rather than literal. Avoid obvious tango cliches such as roses, ballroom costumes, stage spotlights, or poster-style dancing poses. If human forms appear, keep them sparse, elegant, and partially abstracted, like silhouettes, shadows, or sculptural gestures. The image should feel intelligent, mysterious, controlled, and alive.

Preferred palette:
cream, parchment white, charcoal, smoked indigo, dark oxblood, muted brass

Composition notes:
- portrait 2:3 front cover
- strong central visual logic
- generous clean negative space for later typography overlay
- tactile texture, like paper, pigment, ash, graphite, or worn cloth
- subtle sense of fourfold rhythm or quadripartite structure
- elegant contrast between stillness and motion

Tone:
literary, philosophical, modernist, beautiful, restrained, serious, memorable

Avoid:
watermarks, mockup scenes, stock-photo cliches, literal Latin calligraphy, cheesy fantasy effects, neon nightclub aesthetics, clutter, distorted anatomy, messy collage, visible text, fake publisher badges

Front cover only. No spine. No back cover. No watermark.
