Design a polished front-cover image for a literary-philosophical book titled "Quod Tango Muto".

Treat the title as design context only. Do not render any visible words, letters, numbers, logos, or publisher marks inside the artwork.

Core meaning to express:
- what I touch, I change
- transformation through contact
- a four-part rhythm or structure
- controlled motion under pressure
- intimacy without sentimentality
- a serious bookstore-ready literary object

Visual direction:
Create an elegant editorial image built from four interacting forms around a charged center, where contact visibly alters material. The scene should feel like transformation caught mid-process: edges shift, surfaces bruise into new colors, shapes echo each other, and balance is created through tension instead of symmetry. The image should suggest choreography, philosophy, consequence, and metamorphosis.

Avoid a literal dance poster. No ballroom costumes, roses, stage lights, theatrical poses, or obvious tango iconography. If bodies appear, keep them ambiguous and sculptural, almost like charcoal apparitions, ink gestures, or partially erased figures. The result should feel like a modern literary cover from a serious independent press.

Preferred palette:
bone white, soft black, graphite, dark plum, oxidized copper, muted ember

Composition notes:
- portrait 2:3 front cover
- one strong central visual event
- large clean negative space for later typography
- tactile paper and pigment texture
- restrained but memorable contrast
- subtle fourfold structure without diagrammatic literalism
- beauty through precision, not ornament

Tone:
lyrical, severe, modernist, intimate, philosophical, elegant, unforgettable

Avoid:
watermarks, mockup scenes, stock-photo cliches, visible Latin text, poster typography, fantasy glow effects, messy collage, cheesy surrealism, clutter, cheap symmetry

Front cover only. No spine. No back cover. No watermark.
