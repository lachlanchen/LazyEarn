# Quad Tango Muto

_A manuscript scaffold for later writing_

_Author: LazyingArt LLC_

## Opening position

This file is the working foundation for the future book **Quad Tango Muto**.

It is designed to stay simple at the start while giving you enough structure to begin writing immediately, expand chapters later, and collect sources without losing the shape of the manuscript.

## Core prompts

- What is this book really about?
- What does the title **Quad Tango Muto** imply in tone, movement, tension, or structure?
- What should a reader understand, feel, or be able to do after finishing it?
- What kinds of evidence belong in the book: narrative scenes, history, philosophy, technical explanation, memoir, criticism, or synthesis?
- Which ideas deserve full chapters and which should remain motifs, interludes, or notes?

## Book promise

Write one short paragraph here later that answers:

> Why does this book need to exist, and why should someone read it now?

## Draft architecture

### 1. Opening movement

Introduce the title, tone, and main tension.

### 2. Foundations

Define the key terms, assumptions, and world of the book.

### 3. Main development

Build the principal argument, story, investigation, or sequence of transformations.

### 4. Friction and counterforce

Record objections, ambiguities, contradictions, rival views, or internal limits.

### 5. Resolution

Clarify what remains true after the full journey of the manuscript.

### 6. Appendix or notes

Keep source traces, fragments, alternate passages, and unresolved material here until they earn a proper place in the manuscript.
