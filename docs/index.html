This field guide is the repository core of money-and-wealth research. The same mechanism sequence appears in markdown, publication-grade TeX/PDF, and this website. Follow the Chapter 9 path below, then use each matched asset card with the same evidence gates before any allocation or debt-timing conclusion.

  • Distinction-first answers on money creation, ownership, leverage, inequality, and risk.
  • Primary-source coverage across Fed, NY Fed, BLS, Census, IMF, BIS, World Bank, OECD, and SEC resources.
  • Question -> evidence -> claim workflow with SQ-5 source quality gates.
  • Operational additions: household stress dashboard, credit-conditions transmission check, and QE-5/HS-8/LL-6/DP-5 methods for decision-ready monitoring.
  • Latest additions: a channel-map lens from system credit to household outcomes, plus EV-7 entry-vs-incumbent tests for ownership access timing.
  • Cycle 007-010 sync: liquidity-and-cycle clock checks (H.4.1/H.8/NFCI/TIC/MTS/ECI/NBER), entry-and-property-price pulse checks (BFS/BDS/RPP/CPP/GLI/LPC/STEO), credit-access-and-burden bridge checks (DDP/SCE Credit Access/CEX PUMD/SIPP/OECD), and cadence-aware stress classification checks (release calendar/chargeoff/G.17/FSIC/SOI PUF) with EP-6, CAB-9, and CAS-10 wiring.
  • Cycle 011 extension: `9.10 Constraint-first lens for money-growth episodes`, plus the new HC-5 historical-case conversion method to connect major episodes (for example Jekyll Island, Great Depression, Great Inflation, 1973-74 Oil Shock) to actionable ownership decisions.
  • Cycle 012-023 extension: `9.11` Money, debt, and physical constraints decision map, `9.10.1` Great Recession transmission, `9.11.1` historical constraint test on 1973-74 transmission lag, `9.11.4` irreversibility gate, `9.11.5` historical mechanism case (1930-33), `9.11.6` policy-continuity vs ownership-access check, and `9.11.7` portability drill for jurisdictional transfer quality.
