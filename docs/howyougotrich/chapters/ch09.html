Pair the arithmetic with human evidence. Why did people return? What did they expect? Who was excluded or harmed? Did incentives distort a referral? Which complaint repeats? What happens when the channel disappears? A business that cannot answer without a dashboard has measured movement while losing meaning.

計算(けいさん)には、人(ひと)についての証拠(しょうこ)を組み合わせる(くみあわせる)。なぜ人々(ひとびと)は戻っ(もどっ)てきたのか？ 何(なん)を期待(きたい)していたのか？ 誰(だれ)が排除(はいじょ)され、あるいは害(がい)を受け(うけ)たのか？ インセンティブによって紹介(しょうかい)が歪め(ゆがめ)られたか？ どの苦情(くじょう)が繰り返さ(くりかえさ)れているか？ チャネルが消え(きえ)たら何(なん)が起きる(おきる)か？ ダッシュボードなしでは答え(こたえ)られない事業(じぎょう)は、動き(うごき)を測る(はかる)一方(いっぽう)で意味(いみ)を失っ(うしなっ)ている。

Close the value record with one real route

Close Record 2 by adding the channel, the evidence that earned attention and trust, the decision and terms, the delivered result, the response after delivery, and the next evidence step. Record non-return, complaint, cancellation, repair, and exit as carefully as a purchase or referral.

For every stage, name its responsible person, promised completion time, actual waiting time, current constraint, and the person who waits, pays, or bears risk while work is unfinished.
