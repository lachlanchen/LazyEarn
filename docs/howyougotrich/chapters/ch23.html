This disagreement is more useful than a verdict. “Are you happy?” contains several questions: Are your needs met? Do you experience pleasure? Are you content with what you have? The distinctions developed throughout this book keep those answers from being forced into one score. Abundance asks whether a person has sufficient resources, capabilities, relationships, time, and options. Fulfillment asks whether effort is serving purposes that make the work and life worth inhabiting. Contentment asks whether the person can receive what is present and recognize enough, rather than remaining governed by the next comparison.
