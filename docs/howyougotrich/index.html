THE DESTINATION CHANGES THE ROUTE

Three meanings of a richer life

Financial freedom is useful because it can create room for all three. It cannot decide how that room should be used.

01 · 富足

Abundance

Enough resources, capability, relationships, time, resilience, and options to live without chronic scarcity.

02 · 满足

Fulfillment

Directing work and life toward love, service, mastery, and purposes that remain worth the effort.

03 · 知足

Contentment

Retaining the freedom to recognize enough, so ambition remains chosen and accumulation does not take command.
