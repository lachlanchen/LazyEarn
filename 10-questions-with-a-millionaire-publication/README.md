# 10 Questions With a Millionaire

Published LazyEarn edition of the School of Hard Knocks millionaire interview sequence.

This shelf contains two editions:

- `10 Questions With a Millionaire` keeps the lecture-by-lecture notes as a practical interview field book: each chapter studies one conversation for evidence about wealth, ownership, cash flow, risk, sales, delegation, and business judgment.
- `Ten Questions That Build Wealth` reorganizes the same interview evidence into a dynamic question-led field book.
