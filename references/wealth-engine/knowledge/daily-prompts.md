# Daily Prompts (14-day pack)

Updated: 2026-04-05
Purpose: keep study cadence consistent and map each short session to a question-bank ID.

Use with `DP-5` in `knowledge/methods.md`.

| Day | question_id | Prompt | Suggested output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `M1` | Where did credit expand first in the latest cycle, and who likely benefited first? | one channel map note |
| 2 | `M2` | Which moved faster in your target period: asset prices, CPI, or debt-service burden? | one level-vs-rate comparison |
| 3 | `M3` | Did fiscal expansion offset private credit weakness in that period? | one offset test note |
| 4 | `O1` | Which ownership form in your context has the best cash-flow durability per hour of effort? | one ownership ranking row |
| 5 | `O2` | What does median vs mean ownership show in your chosen population? | one distribution caution line |
| 6 | `L1` | Which debt threshold appears before delinquency transitions rise? | one trigger threshold draft |
| 7 | `L2` | Under what housing-price and income assumptions does mortgage leverage turn fragile? | one stress-test assumption set |
| 8 | `I1` | How much of observed inequality is ownership concentration versus earnings dispersion? | one decomposition hypothesis |
| 9 | `R1` | Which risk in your plan is volatility, and which could cause permanent capital impairment? | one risk split note |
| 10 | `R2` | What explicit rule would have reduced blow-up odds in the last boom-bust episode? | one rule draft |
| 11 | `R3` | Which stage (survival to autonomy) describes you now, and what allocation rule fits that stage? | one stage-based allocation note |
| 12 | `H1` | Which historical regime most resembles current credit and inflation structure? | one regime match memo line |
| 13 | `H2` | Which institution appears most linked to broad ownership in your country set? | one institution-evidence pair |
| 14 | `H3` | What one metric can combine net worth trend, liquidity, and optionality for your dashboard? | one composite metric proposal |
