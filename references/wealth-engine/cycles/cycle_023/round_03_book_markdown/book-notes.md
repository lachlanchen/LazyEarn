## Chapter edit made

- Added `### 9.11.7 Portability drill for policy narratives` in `investment/wealth-from-first-principles.md`.
- Added a concrete two-channel test for policy portability:
  - Policy continuity channel
  - Ownership access channel
- Added decision-use sequence:
  1. Keep leverage defensive when continuity improves but access does not.
  2. Resume controlled scaling only after two consecutive windows improve across access and burden.
  3. Hold optionality when physical capacity or labor-cost pressure stays constrained.
