# Cycle 013 Round 2 questions (question_bank)

Date: 2026-04-06

## Learning agenda
Refine current research around five unresolved frontiers: money-creation pathways, durable ownership versus leverage, inequality and ownership participation, irreversible loss, and historical transmission memory.

## Cycle 013 question set

| ID | Theme | Sharp question | Evidence anchor set |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U59 | Money creation pathway | Which credit/use channels receive the first measurable share of net money/credit expansion: businesses, households, mortgages, or asset-backed credit? | Fed H.6, Fed Z.1, Fed SLOOS, NY Fed SCE |
| U60 | Constraint-resilient wealth | Do leverage-heavy wealth methods fail when capacity/utilization or energy rails are weak even if asset prices rise? | EIA STEO, EIA MER, BEA Fixed Assets, Fed DSR/FOR |
| U61 | Inequality mechanism | Are inequality gains from expansion phases driven by broader ownership entry or by valuation concentration among incumbents? | Fed DFA, OECD IDD/WDD, CBO LTBO, Census wealth tables |
| U62 | Irreversible risk | Which losses are reversible volatility versus durable ownership-loss (control, legal tenure, forced-sale path)? | CFPB MEMS, HMDA modified LAR, FDIC unbanked survey, SEC filing evidence |
| U63 | Historical continuity | Which historical institutional designs still shape channel routing in modern credit transmission (for example post-crisis redesigns)? | IMF Article IV releases, Federal Reserve historical essays, BIS Quarterly Review |

## Immediate testing rules

- U59 should use one stock metric and one disaggregated flow channel metric before any conclusion.
- U60 should add at least one physical-capacity/throughput and one debt-burden series per test window.
- U61 must always separate participation and valuation before concluding inequality mechanism.
- U62 should include one reversible indicator and one irreversible-control proxy before assigning regime risk.
- U63 should pair at least one historical anchor with one current-cycle release alignment before interpretation.
