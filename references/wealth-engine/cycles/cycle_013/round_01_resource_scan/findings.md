# Cycle 013 Round 1 findings: resource_scan

Date: 2026-04-06

## Scope
This round focused on high-quality official and primary sources in the money/wealth/investing and inequality space, with emphasis on up-to-date policy-relevant data and institutions that support richer narrative chapters.

## Sources captured
- Federal Reserve Board: Z.1 distributional and flow-of-funds infrastructure + Beige Book regional economic commentary.
- U.S. BEA: Personal Income and Outlays + NIPA handbook for macro definitions.
- IMF: Article IV public documentation, with a current U.S. consultation press release for cross-country policy comparison.
- EIA: Short-Term Energy Outlook and AEO retrospective material for production constraints and forward price dynamics.
- OECD: Income and Wealth Distribution Database for inequality baselines.
- CBO: Long-Term Budget Outlook executive summary for debt and fiscal dynamics.
- BIS and UNCTAD: International finance and global FDI context.
- CFPB + Berkshire Hathaway annual reporting for investor behavior and applied capital-allocation perspective.

## What was useful this round
- The richest immediate additions were institutional pages that already expose release dates and revision cadence, reducing risk of stale links.
- EIA + BEA + Fed + CBO together give a strong chain from production, through income, to fiscal and financial stability outcomes.
- IMF Article IV feeds are useful for short case-study inserts and to frame policy choice under constraint.

## Open issues for next scan rounds
- A deeper pass is needed on IMF staff report PDFs by DOI for selected countries with explicit macro datasets.
- For the investor behavior track, we can add one or two SEC/EDGAR-linked historical 10-K examples if we want stronger company-level source depth.
