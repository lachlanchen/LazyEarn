# Cycle 003 Round 02 Questions: question_bank

Date: 2026-04-05

## Objective
Convert the expanded cycle_003 source set into sharper, falsifiable question structure for money creation, ownership distribution, leverage risk, and durable wealth methods.

## Distinctions added

- `Credit price vs credit quantity`: lower policy rates do not guarantee household/business access if lending standards tighten.
- `Stated expectations vs revealed behavior`: survey sentiment can lead, but realized borrowing/spending validates signal quality.
- `Gross income share vs after-tax disposable share`: distribution interpretation changes after taxes/transfers are accounted for.

## Tier 1 anchor improvements

- `M1`: added Fed SLOOS to link macro credit creation with transmission/access channels.
- `L1`: expanded to include Fed DSR/FOR and CFPB Consumer Credit Trends for threshold and flow context.
- `I1`: expanded with BEA DPI, Census CPS income tables, and IRS SOI for stronger income-vs-ownership decomposition.
- `R1`: added Fed SHED to connect market/credit conditions to household fragility outcomes.
