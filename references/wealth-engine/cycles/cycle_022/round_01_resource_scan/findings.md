# Cycle 022 Round 1 findings

Date: 2026-04-06

Objective: add fresh, high-quality wealth-and-money sources for money, wealth, historical episodes, inequality, investing context, and production/constraint material.

Sources added in this round:
- Congressional Budget Office — The Budget and Economic Outlook: 2026 to 2036
  - URL: https://www.cbo.gov/system/files/2026-02/61882-Outlook-2026.pdf
  - Value: official multi-decade fiscal projection with debt and deficit paths; directly useful for long-horizon wealth/distribution arguments.
- Federal Reserve History — The Great Recession and Its Aftermath (PDF)
  - URL: https://www.federalreservehistory.org/-/media/Project/FedHistory/FedHistory/Documents/essaysPDFs/The-Great-Recession-and-Its-Aftermath-_-Federal-Reserve-History.pdf
  - Value: concise institutional history and transmission narrative from the 2007-09 crisis period.
- Bank for International Settlements — Annual Economic Report 2025
  - URL: https://www.bis.org/publ/arpdf/ar2025e.htm
  - Value: global financial architecture and policy context for macro stability comparisons.
- UNCTAD — State of Commodity Dependence 2025
  - URL: https://unctad.org/system/files/official-document/ditccom2025d3_en.pdf
  - Value: commodity concentration and dependence structure useful for production-capacity constraint sections.
- OECD — To Have and Have Not: How to Bridge the Gap in Opportunities
  - URL: https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2025/09/to-have-and-have-not-how-to-bridge-the-gap-in-opportunities_f642138a/dec143ad-en.pdf
  - Value: strong inequality and wealth-opportunity treatment, especially transfer and intergenerational accumulation mechanics.
- U.S. Geological Survey — Mineral Commodity Summaries 2025
  - URL: https://pubs.usgs.gov/periodicals/mcs2025/mcs2025.pdf
  - Value: production-side evidence on critical minerals, import reliance, and capacity constraints.
