# Cycle 001 Round 02 Questions

Date: 2026-04-05

## Focus shortlist (next two rounds)

1. M1: Through which sectors does new bank credit enter first, and who receives it?
2. M2: What share of liquidity growth flows into assets versus CPI versus debt service relief?
3. O1: Which ownership forms create repeatable cash flow with the least ongoing labor?
4. O2: How concentrated are productive assets by percentile, age, and household type?
5. L1: What leverage or debt-service thresholds best predict delinquency transitions?
6. L2: When does mortgage leverage improve outcomes versus increase fragility?
7. I1: How much inequality comes from ownership gaps versus earnings gaps?
8. R1: Which household risks are true ruin risks rather than tolerable volatility?
9. R3: How should asset allocation change by stage (survival to autonomy)?

## Distinctions this shortlist forces

- Money creation is not the same as money distribution.
- Income growth is not the same as ownership growth.
- Volatility is not the same as insolvency.
- Leverage for productivity is not the same as leverage for consumption.
- Median household outcomes are not the same as mean aggregate outcomes.
