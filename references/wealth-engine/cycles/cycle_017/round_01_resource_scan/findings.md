# Cycle 017 round 01 findings: high-signal resource additions

## Focus
This scan prioritized institutional history, credit-market friction signals, distributional mechanisms, and production constraints to support money-and-wealth sections that still need stronger historical and physical grounding.

- **Financial history (1930s system stress episodes)**: Federal Reserve historical essays and PDFs on the 1930-31 panics, the 1933 bank holiday, and the Emergency Banking Act were selected to support section-level material on liquidity runs, payment breakdowns, and forced-confidence restoration.
- **Credit-facility tension (2026)**: ECB January 2026 bank-lending-survey press release and BIS March 2026 macro-financial review were added for current transmission and risk-pricing contrasts.
- **Monetary architecture and infrastructure**: BIS next-generation monetary and financial system press release was added to support a constrained-rail discussion on how payment/money rails alter risk and access.
- **Wealth/inequality architecture**: OECD 2025 household-wealth trend paper and household-distribution methodology handbook were added for chapter sections where mechanism claims need micro-macro reconciliation.
- **Wealth transfer policy**: OECD inheritance taxation page added as an institution-level source for persistence and access asymmetry.
- **Physical constraint anchors**: IEA Oil Market Report 2026 and IEA WEO 2025 free dataset added for production throughput, energy pressure, and macro-finance transmission examples.

## Freshness and evidence quality checks
- **Freshness window target**: 2026-01 to 2026-04 releases were prioritized for current-policy and current-balance-sheet context.
