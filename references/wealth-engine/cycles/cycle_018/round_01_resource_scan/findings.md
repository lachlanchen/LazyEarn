Find high-quality, source-backed, and current materials on money/wealth mechanisms, financial stability, wealth measurement, and household access channels with strong institutional grounding.

## Rationale for inclusion
- These sources are official or near-primary, include explicit publication/update context, and support mechanism-first sections in the book:
  - monetary transmission and policy trade-offs,
  - household and small-firm access under stress,
  - cross-country comparability of wealth definitions,
  - measurable behavior signals that can become decision thresholds.
- The non-U.S. sources help prevent U.S.-only causal framing and give stronger historical comparison for sections 3, 5, 8, and 9.
