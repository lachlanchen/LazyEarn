- FDIC Quarterly Banking Profile (including Q4 2025 release PDF page).

## Why these were prioritized
- These sources are official, release/cycle-based, and directly relevant to Chapter 9 transmission logic:
  - financing cost and credit-rate conditions (H.15)
  - bank balance-sheet and loan/deposit quality indicators (FDIC QBP)
  - failure handling, coordination lag, and policy sequencing (GAO + FSB)
  - non-bank and repo-channel fragility where bank-centric transmission is incomplete.
- They expand coverage beyond purely banking-deposit channels and support decision-gate writing for liquidity and coordination delays.
