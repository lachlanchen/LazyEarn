# How You Got Successful? book order

This is the lecture-by-lecture order used for the Jim Rohn course book inside `LazyEarn`.

It is intentionally not the raw playlist order. The goal is to make the compiled book read more like a gradual companion volume:

1. start with internal obstacles and self-command
2. move into planning, journaling, and time use
3. widen into ambition, seriousness, and personal agency
4. only then move toward value, income, and wealth language
5. place the more applied seminar and sales material later
6. end with the `2009 Jim Rohn Last Speech` as an epilogue

## Why this order is smoother

- `Diseases of Attitude` is the right opening because it names the internal failure modes before any tactic appears.
- `Have A Solid Plan`, `How to use a Journal`, and `Time Is Valuable` form the operational backbone early, so the reader gets a concrete discipline scaffold before the motivational material expands.
- The ambition cluster then escalates the psychological stakes without immediately flattening into money slogans.
- The value-and-wealth cluster comes later, once the book has already established agency, seriousness, and self-command.
- The network-marketing and seminar-heavy pieces remain in the book, but they sit later so they read as applied extensions rather than as the whole meaning of success.
- The `2009` final speech works better as a closing frame than as an early chapter because it carries retrospective and farewell energy.
