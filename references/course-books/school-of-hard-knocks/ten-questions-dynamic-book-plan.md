# Ten Questions That Build Wealth

This is the rough dynamic-book order for the `10 Questions with a Millionaire`
playlist.

The source set currently contains 22 transcript/subtitle pairs under:

- `markdown/lazyearn/school-of-hard-knocks/10-questions-with-a-millionaire`
- `subtitles/lazyearn/school-of-hard-knocks/10-questions-with-a-millionaire`

The book should not follow video order. It should preserve the recurring
interview format as a union of questions, then distribute each interview's
stories, numbers, contradictions, and mechanisms into the best-fitting question.

## Rough Question Spine

1. What was the first real money-making mechanism?
2. What painful bottleneck or customer problem created the opportunity?
3. What did the person actually own or control?
4. How did customers, distribution, reputation, or attention arrive?
5. How was risk financed, survived, or transferred?
6. Which skill or operating discipline compounded fastest?
7. How did income become enterprise value, equity, or durable wealth?
8. What did taxes, leverage, legal structure, or financial engineering change?
9. What failure, loss, or humiliation taught the rule?
10. Which relationships, networks, trust, or teams mattered?
11. What advice would survive for a beginner or younger self?
12. What is wealth for after the number is reached?

## Writing Policy

- Keep a story-first opening whenever an interview gives a vivid scene, reversal,
  or number.
- Preserve concrete claims and numbers, but keep revenue, profit, personal cash,
  deal value, equity value, and net worth distinct.
- Prefer adding evidence to the right question chapter over creating a new
  chapter for each video.
- Repetition is allowed when a new interview gives a new witness, case, number,
  contradiction, or sharper phrasing.
- Structural changes should be conservative and tied to new evidence from the
  current lecture.
- Use playlist-1 duplicate markdown/subtitle material when the same video
  appears in multiple School of Hard Knocks playlists.
