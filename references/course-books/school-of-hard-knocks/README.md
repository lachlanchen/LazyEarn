# School of Hard Knocks source notes

This folder stores book-preparation notes for interview-style YouTube sources from `School of Hard Knocks`.

These are not transcripts yet. They are source dossiers meant to help with:

- deciding whether a video is worth a full `Video2Book` pass
- identifying the strongest book angles before writing starts
- separating anecdotal interview material from claims that need outside verification

## Current notes

- [Asking A Billionaire Lawyer How To Make $1,000,000!](asking-a-billionaire-lawyer-how-to-make-1000000.md)
- [Asking Billionaires If They Pay Taxes!](asking-billionaires-if-they-pay-taxes.md)

## Why these notes matter

Interview videos can be useful book seeds, but they are not enough on their own. A good book needs:

- the interview narrative
- the institutional and historical background
- independent facts, numbers, and definitions
- a clear frame that turns scattered remarks into a coherent argument

These notes are designed to make that second step easier.
