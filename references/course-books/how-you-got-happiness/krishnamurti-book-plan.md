# How You Got Happiness? Krishnamurti Book Plan

This plan turns the three completed Krishnamurti transcript sets into one sequential book-writing queue.

Working title:

- `How You Got Happiness?`

Core source:

- J. Krishnamurti, official Krishnamurti Foundation Trust video material.
- Source playlists already downloaded and transcribed under `lazyearn/jiddu-krishnamurti`.
- Curated and typeset by LazyingArt LLC with Video2Book.

## Editorial Thesis

The book should not sell happiness as a technique, mood, achievement, or belief system.

The governing question is:

- What remains of happiness when pleasure, fear, dependence, image, authority, and self-improvement theater are seen clearly?

The book should preserve Krishnamurti's radical edge while making the inquiry readable:

- source-conscious, not devotional
- clear, not slogan-like
- philosophical, but grounded in the talks
- interested in happiness only through attention, freedom, relationship, love, and the ending of psychological conflict

## Three-Part Structure

### Part I: The False Map Of Happiness

Purpose:

- Begin with the reader's ordinary assumption that happiness can be pursued.
- Show why pleasure, knowledge, image, responsibility, and disorder complicate that assumption.
- Establish observation and self-knowledge as the beginning of the inquiry.

Chapters:

1. Does Pleasure Bring Happiness
2. Knowledge And The Transformation Of Man
3. Learning About Oneself
4. Knowledge And Conflict In Relationship
5. What Communication Requires
6. The Responsible Human Being
7. Order From Understanding Disorder
8. The Question Of Transformation
9. Seeing What We Are

### Part II: Fear, Desire, Hurt, Love, Death

Purpose:

- Move from the false map into the emotional machinery that keeps happiness unstable.
- Treat fear, desire, hurt, sorrow, sex, love, death, and violence as linked structures rather than isolated topics.
- Keep repetition when a later talk adds a new angle or a fresh distinction.

Chapters:

10. Can Fear End
11. The Nature And Eradication Of Fear
12. Understanding Desire
13. Being Hurt And Hurting Others
14. Sorrow Passion And Beauty
15. Love Sex And Pleasure
16. Death Life And Love
17. Freedom From Violence

### Part III: Attention, Meditation, Transformation, The Sacred

Purpose:

- Turn from diagnosis toward a different quality of living.
- Make listening, meditation, religion, education, aloneness, image, and sacredness the culmination, not decorative afterwords.
- End with transformation and sacred life rather than with a method checklist.

Chapters:

18. The Art Of Listening
19. Meditation Requires Order
20. What Is Meditation
21. Attention That Pervades Life
22. Meditation And The Sacred Mind
23. Religion Authority And Education I
24. Religion Authority And Education II
25. A Different Way Of Living
26. The Mechanical Way Of Living
27. Can I Completely Change
28. Aloneness And Freedom
29. The Image Of Yourself
30. The Ending Of Image
31. Life Is Sacred

## Writing Rules For The Notes Worker

- Follow `krishnamurti-book-order.tsv` exactly for the sequential queue.
- Use the current transcript as the primary evidence for the current chapter.
- Keep the whole three-part plan in mind while writing each chapter.
- Do not turn Krishnamurti into ordinary self-help, productivity advice, therapy content, or a method system.
- Separate observation, claim, implication, and interpretation.
- Preserve useful repetition when it represents a new talk, new angle, or new supporting evidence.
- Use diagrams sparingly and only when they clarify relationships such as fear/time/thought, pleasure/desire, image/hurt, or attention/meditation.
- Keep LaTeX tables and diagrams pocket-safe: no wide flowcharts, no long unwrapped labels, no over-wide equations.
- Credit J. Krishnamurti, the Krishnamurti Foundation Trust, and LazyingArt LLC in front matter, not as repeated body filler.
